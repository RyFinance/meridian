# Cycle 8 — Canadian account and implementation evidence

Research only. Sources opened/searched 2026-09-28. This is external-source research, not portfolio performance evidence or an executable trading instruction. Current fees/rules are not claimed to have prevailed throughout the historical simulation. No portfolio returns were computed for this note.

## Financing and liquidation model inputs

[IBKR Canada financing](https://www.interactivebrokers.ca/en/index.php?f=46803) quotes smallest retail debit tiers at its benchmark plus 1.50%: USD through $100,000 and CAD through $130,000. Benchmark has a zero floor, daily accrual, monthly posting. Its benchmark is not literally the central-bank target: [benchmark reference](https://www.interactivebrokers.ca/en/index.php?f=46383&p=i) displayed USD 3.63%, CAD 2.131% effective 2026-08-26. Use historical effective federal funds +150 bp for USD and Bank of Canada policy rate +150 bp for CAD as **declared counterfactual reconstruction**, not verified historical broker rates. Fix any high-cost sensitivity before returns, e.g. policy +350 bp. No claim of a verified full 1970-present retail rate history is justified.

[Questrade margin pricing](https://corporatefx.questrade.com/pricing/self-directed-commissions-plans-fees/margin-interest) gives debit balances below $100,000 CAD prime+4.25% and USD prime+4.50%; larger balances prime+3.25% and prime+3.50%. Displayed prime rates were CAD4.45% (2025-10-30), USD7.00% (2026-09-17). These are prime-based, so do not call these spreads over central-bank policy. Broker disclosures allow liquidation without notice. Special high-volume options discounts are irrelevant to a passive ETF investor.

[IBKR's Canada-resident margin schedule](https://www.interactivebrokers.co.uk/en/trading/margin-stocks.php?ex=ca&hm=ca&pm=0&rgt=1&rsk=0&rst=080804010401010801) distinguishes ordinary 50% margin and eligible reduced-margin US instruments at 30%; house requirements can increase. This is the broker's Canada-resident table served from its UK hostname, with legacy IIROC terminology. It does not verify each ETF's live house requirement. Treat 30% as an explicit eligible-instrument assumption, 50% as stress. Leveraged ETF requirement is min(base requirement × fund leverage,100%); do not apply ordinary 30% collateral to TMF/UPRO. Portfolio margin is not available to IBKR Canada customers under this table.

Suggested preregistered path rule: debit accrues daily in its own currency; mark assets/debt and assess equity/assets against maintenance daily, before rebalance. At first breach liquidate to cash, charge declared stress execution cost and retain the actual resulting equity (or insolvency), then cash thereafter; never restart a rescued portfolio. Report one forced-liquidation event and its date/outcome separately from unconstrained drawdown. Daily-close detection misses intraday triggers and gaps: the measured event count is a lower-bound approximation. Repeat under 50% maintenance; at 2x initial gross exposure the account starts exactly at 50%, leaving essentially no cushion. A margin call count without this realized path would overstate survivability.

USD-debt and CAD-debt simulations must remain distinct. For CAD financing of USD assets, debt stays CAD; CAD asset value moves with CAD per USD. Simply charging a lower CAD rate against USD returns omits currency exposure. USD financing converted to CAD reporting similarly requires translating the entire net portfolio, not just assets.

## Account feasibility and distribution taxes

[IBKR registered-account rules](https://www.interactivebrokers.ca/en/accounts/rsp_tfsa_information.php) prohibit margin/debits in TFSA and RRSP; purchases must be fully funded in their currency. USD/CAD conversion is permitted. Ordinary unlevered ETFs are feasible; internal leverage within an eligible ETF does not require account margin. Thus P1/P2/P3/P5 at 1x and cash are structurally feasible. Externally levered versions are not. P4 literal 60/40+10+10 sums to120% and is not funded at100%; the preregistration must specify normalization or an internal-leverage implementation. Fully paid NTSX/RSSB/RSST/GDE and SSO/UPRO/TMF can express internal leverage subject to broker eligibility, but are not exact replicas of every synthetic menu exposure. General eligibility is not a verified permission for this owner's account.

[BlackRock Canada withholding guide](https://www.blackrock.com/ca/individual/en/literature/brochure/withholding-tax-reference-guide-en-ca.pdf) distinguishes direct US ETF ownership: US dividend withholding is generally15% for Canadian treaty investors; TFSA withholding is a net cost, whereas directly held US ETFs in RRSP/RRIF generally receive the US pension exemption. Taxable-account withholding is generally creditable; do not subtract both full withholding and full domestic dividend tax without modeling the credit. Qualified US bond interest is exempt, including US ETF distributions assumed wholly qualified interest. Apply tax drag to taxable distributions, never15% of total return. At a hypothetical2% equity cash dividend yield the TFSA drag is30bp/year, scaled to actual eligible distributions; it is not a fixed30bp for every fund.

GLD's physical gold exposure has no equity dividend stream: do not impose an equity-dividend haircut on its price gains. Futures funds may distribute ordinary income, capital gains, interest-related dividends, and other amounts; tax classification must come from issuer annual distribution records. A blanket15% haircut on all DBMF/KMLM/RSST/GDE distributions is only a conservative sensitivity, not an established tax liability. RSSB's international holdings can incur non-US source withholding inside funds even in an RRSP. RRSP US exemption does not refund that layer. Personal Canadian capital-gain/income tax, RRSP withdrawal tax, contribution-room effects, and tax-deductibility of margin interest require owner-specific assumptions and are outside a generic pretax wealth comparison.

## Trading and FX costs

[IBKR manual spot FX](https://www.interactivebrokers.ca/en/pricing/commissions-spot-currencies.php): commission max(0.20bp × trade value,USD2), plus bid/ask spread;20bp is a hundredfold unit error. Apply on entry/exit or contributions actually converted, not annually to the entire portfolio. A research assumption of1bp spread each conversion plus published commission can be declared; it is not a guaranteed fill. Keep USD sale proceeds in USD between rebalances. High retail FX sensitivity can be declared at1.5% each conversion, explicitly hypothetical unless a chosen broker's rate is separately verified.

[IBKR US ETF commissions](https://www.interactivebrokers.ca/en/pricing/commissions-stocks.php?re=amer): fixed$0.005/share, $1 minimum,1% trade-value maximum plus specified regulatory fees; tiered$0.0035/share,$0.35 minimum with venue/clearing/regulatory fees. A flat bps rebalance proxy needs declared account size and cannot be called exact broker pricing. Charge on dollars traded, with buys and sells both counted. Current issuer median full spreads include VTI/IEF/SSO0.01%, RSST0.06%, GDE0.40%; entry at ask or exit at bid costs approximately half the full spread before market impact. GDE's observed spread is40bp full, not4bp. Historical crisis spreads can be substantially wider; current medians do not reconstruct them.

The daily-reset model must compound each day's leveraged underlying return and subtract expense and financing once. A declared policy/T-bill +50bp swap-financing spread is a modeling assumption; a universal historical swap spread was not verified. Fees exclude important financing costs: Direxion expressly excludes swap financing and related costs from its operating-expense limit. Real ETF NAV/adjusted-total-return data already incorporate fund fees/financing; subtracting those again double-counts. Validate synthetic SSO/UPRO/TMF against actual NAV total returns and distributions. Both ProShares pages identify4pmET NAV calculation; this does not establish the LSE data convention.

Within the exact named menu, DBMF has a lower stated expense than KMLM, and VTI lower than SPY. They do not have identical exposures. For reference only, State Street's opened fund list shows GLDM0.10%, a cheaper gold wrapper, but replacing GLD would add an unregistered implementation ticker and must not alter the evaluated menu. No all-broker universal 'cheapest' claim follows from these observations: FX size, commissions, spreads, account type, and financing dominate differently. IBKR is the lower financing-cost broker of the two verified ordinary retail schedules. RRSP directly held US equity ETFs avoids the TFSA dividend leakage; external leverage requires a nonregistered margin account.
