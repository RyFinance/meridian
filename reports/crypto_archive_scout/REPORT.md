## 2020–2026 and pre-2020 coverage

- **2020–2026, survivor-free, keyless, and demonstrably includes delisted contracts:** **Bybit** (linear USDT perps, LUNAUSDT confirmed present in static archive + funding API) and **BitMEX** (though BitMEX's own delisted-contract roster is different from Binance's — it's built around dated inverse futures and a smaller perp set, not LUNA/FTT specifically, since neither LUNA nor FTT perps were ever listed on BitMEX under those names in our check).
- **Pre-2020:** **BitMEX only**, confirmed back to **2014-11-22** by downloading and decoding an actual archive file (`trade_20141122.csv.gz`, contains `XBTZ14` trades). No other venue in this set has pre-2020 keyless-accessible history (OKX and Kraken Futures existed pre-2020 but a working bulk/deep-history endpoint wasn't confirmed; Deribit's `expired=true` endpoint looks shallow rather than archival; Hyperliquid and dYdX v4 didn't exist yet).
- **OKX and Kraken Futures** are plausible pre-2020 venues by history (OKX perps since ~2018, Kraken Futures/Crypto Facilities since 2018) but this keyless probe could not confirm a deep, enumerable, survivor-free path to that history — OKX's static bulk-download candidates all 404'd, and Kraken's delisted-contract enumeration would require the separate CSV portal rather than the REST API tested here.

## Survivorship verdicts

- **BitMEX: strong pass.** Day-indexed, non-symbol-filtered S3 archive is survivor-free by construction; delisted contracts enumerable via `instrument?filter=state:Settled`. Verified with a real downloaded/decoded sample from launch day.
- **Bybit: pass, with caveats.** Confirmed LUNAUSDT retained in both the static archive and the funding-history API post-delisting. Funding-history API is windowed (needs pagination for a full series), but the static archive is the more reliable survivor-free backbone.
- **dYdX v4: pass, but short history.** Clean `status` field for delisted/settled markets, but only from October 2023 — not useful for a 2020–2022 replication, and not the Binance-comparable period the funding-XS test needs.
- **Deribit: inconclusive/likely fail for depth.** `expired=true` is the intended mechanism but returned suspiciously few historical instruments in our probe — looks like limited lookback rather than full archival retention. Would need more probing (per-quarter symbol guesses) to confirm depth; not confirmed as survivor-free across multiple years from this pass.
- **OKX, Kraken Futures: inconclusive.** Live instrument lists appear survivor-filtered (current-only), and no working bulk/enumerable delisted-archive path was found via keyless REST alone in this session.
- **Hyperliquid: N/A for the relevant period.** Too young (2023-05+) to matter for a LUNA/FTX-era replication; would only be useful for a *forward*, post-2023 sample.

## Recommended replication venue(s)

1. **Primary recommendation: BitMEX.** It is the only venue here with a *verified*, survivor-free, day-indexed public archive reaching back to inception (2014-11-22), plus a keyless REST API with a clean `Settled`/`Unlisted` filter for delisted contracts, keyless funding history, and no confirmed ToS issue for light, polite access. It predates Binance's own archive by years, giving a genuinely independent (not just parallel) sample, and its inverse-contract, dated-futures history means an external replication would even stress-test the funding-carry mechanism under a structurally different contract design (inverse vs. Binance's linear USDT-margined perps) — a stronger test of generality than a same-design replication would be. Caveat: BitMEX never listed LUNA/FTT-named perps, so the specific 2022 LUNA/FTX survivorship stress test used for Binance would need BitMEX's own delisted-symbol set (dated futures, smaller altcoin perps) rather than a literal LUNA/FTT match.

2. **Secondary/complementary: Bybit.** Confirmed survivor-free coverage of the same LUNA-era stress episode, on a linear-USDT-margined contract design closer to Binance's, from 2020 onward — good as a design-matched replication, whereas BitMEX is a structurally-different one. Between the two, running the replication on **both** (BitMEX for depth/independence, Bybit for design similarity) would be the strongest confirmatory pair.

3. **Not recommended as-is:** OKX and Kraken Futures (survivor-free access not established via keyless probing — would need further work, e.g. locating Kraken's separate historical CSV portal or an OKX bulk endpoint that wasn't tried); Deribit (shallow expired-instrument retention observed, unclear depth); Hyperliquid and dYdX v4 (too young — no 2020–2022 coverage, so cannot replicate the LUNA/FTX-era Binance sample; dYdX v4 could be a *forward*, out-of-sample venue for future research given its clean delisting metadata, but that's a different use case than replication).
