# Audit of the frozen US1 model

Client date: October 1, 2026. This is a post-result audit. The original code, data snapshots and report have not been edited. The follow-up receipt was recorded before the new simulations.

## Consequential findings

1. **The original forward ranking and forward overlay bootstrap use different means.** In `research/cycle8_us_only/run.py:forward`, funded sleeve arithmetic expectation is declared geometric expectation plus half common-window annual variance, less applicable fees. Overlay expectation subtracts cash. In `bootstrap`, XT/XB instead receive the geometric expectation less cash without the variance term. Central XT is therefore 1.0000% annual arithmetic in the original bootstrap versus 1.7730% in ranking; XB is 0.6000% versus 0.8699%, including the 0.15% bond fee. At a 50% overlay, the portfolio arithmetic discrepancy is approximately 0.3865 or 0.1350 percentage points annually at outer 1x. C1 tests this minimal correction. It does not fix every approximation in the forward model.

2. **Funded mean calibration also needs an explicit convention.** The original bootstrap fits annual geometric growth through log shifts, while ranking uses a half-variance arithmetic approximation with a common-window variance. These need not produce identical monthly arithmetic means, especially over different source windows. C2 fits the ranking's monthly arithmetic target exactly and constructs overlay returns from the corresponding funded return minus the same cash. It retains the approximate half-variance ranking and the original cash/financing annual-rate divided by twelve convention. The `moment_checks.csv` file reports implied geometric growth so that this approximation is visible rather than hidden. This is a model consistency correction, not new evidence that the assumptions will occur.

3. **Month-end bootstrap materially understates daily drawdown risk.** The supplement samples entire asset/FX months together, preserving the daily paths within each selected month and running the same liquidation engine daily. It does not replace daily data with straight lines for equity or bonds. Forward mean changes use the original constant within-month log anchoring. Treasury market holidays still use the declared carry/zero-price-change reconstruction; FX observation times are still unsynchronized. The daily supplement adds a relevant tail check but does not certify intraday or official-close risk.

4. **The crisis helper checks the beginning but not the ending of a crisis window.** A synthetic series stopping in March 2022 is incorrectly admitted as a complete January–October 2022 crisis by the original helper. This is a demonstrated coverage bug. No saved original complete-crisis row was found to be affected: old main series extend beyond each endpoint; short funds beginning after a crisis start are already rejected. A strict endpoint and consecutive-month checker is used in the follow-up verification. Original reports are preserved as required.

5. **Account feasibility is provisional.** The original table's factor-based maintenance assumptions are lower-bound illustrations, not a verified security-specific initial-margin schedule. Initial and maintenance requirements are different concepts. House concentration requirements, broker overrides, and whether a stacked fund falls into a particular leveraged-ETF category remain unverified. A field marked `rule_initially_feasible=True` must not be read as permission to open a position. This does not affect the unlevered 60/40 answer, which has no account borrowing. Synthetic future overlays and externally borrowed registered-account rows remain unsuitable as claimed exact TFSA implementations.

6. **Kelly is a diagnostic, not a leverage authorization.** The approximate mean-minus-borrowing-rate over variance formula is applied to each portfolio's 1x realized returns, including embedded exposure in P4 and P6. It does not translate those figures into an underlying notional ceiling, distinguish the lending/borrowing kink below 1x, or guarantee margin survival. The uncertainty bounds remain descriptive. No fixed leverage level was changed based on Kelly.

## Scope and remaining limits

The original USD and CAD historical Sharpe hurdle is retained, including tiny failures. No new portfolio is introduced and no weights are tuned. Current ETF histories cannot represent missing early crises. AQR factor returns are not ETF returns; recent live fund tracking remains loose. A paid or unavailable official source is not bypassed. Original A4 intervals, trading cost assumptions and historic returns are not retrospectively rewritten.

Three agents were dispatched for independent review. One returned the overlay inconsistency before its limit was reached; all then stopped with account usage-limit errors. The coordinator independently reproduced the finding and completed the code, checks and simulations. Their failure does not turn uncompleted source gates into passes.
