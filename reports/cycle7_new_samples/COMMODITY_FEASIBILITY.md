# Cycle 7 commodity trend: new-market feasibility only

Status: source and date-coverage investigation, 2026-09-27. **No signal, strategy, control, or benchmark return was calculated. No Cycle 7 sample is registered here.** This note does not amend the frozen Cycle 6 TSMOM-CM specification or its universe.

## Binding frozen rule

The source is `research/cycle6/specs/TSMOM-CM.md` (snapshot `cycle6_eval_v1`, manifest `ba67379be094135e32557468710a078ea9ac6f0d87516fef331e7788b22d29be`). The 12 original economic markets are Brent, copper, corn, gasoline, gold, natural gas, palladium, platinum, silver, soybeans, wheat, and WTI. A candidate needs daily **excess** returns (not spot prices); ≥756 valid daily observations at the decision close; all 12 preceding monthly excess returns; and the existing class-level ≥3-market floor. Signal = sign of trailing 12-month excess return including month t, no skip. Weight = sign × 0.40 / mean-centred EW daily excess-return volatility (decay 60/61, annualisation 261), equal market average. The gross fidelity shadow is month-end-close to month-end-close; the net implementation has the first-session-close lag and 6 bp/side cost, 3× stress. The frozen AQR `TSMOM^CM` thresholds and always-long exposure-matched control remain unchanged. No alias of an existing market can be a new independent market.

Cycle 6's construction priority for commodities was a contract-spliced continuation where available, otherwise commodity ETF total return minus USD cash; its two EIA-to-ETF splices were WTI and natural gas. **EIA C1 level changes are not valid futures returns across rolls.** The director correction requires held-contract C1/C2 splicing on roll dates; the old “clean as-is” assertion in a commodity data report is superseded. Neither a spot series nor an unspliced front price can be substituted for the frozen excess-return input.

## EIA, exchange settlements, and the no-approximation boundary

- The existing EIA history covers WTI and natural gas as contract-spliced pre-ETF extensions. EIA front prices are not new economic markets and cannot manufacture cocoa/coffee/cotton/livestock returns. Earlier EIA heating-oil/gasoline series have documented gaps/specification transitions and are already overlapping energy markets, not clean additions.
- CME documents official continuous series for soybean oil, lean hogs, live/feeder cattle and others, including a roll identifier; historical files require a DataMine licence. ICE advertises historical settlement reports for coffee, sugar and cocoa through its data service, not a verified public contract-level archive. **Missing exact field** for a futures construction: a historical daily settlement for every held and successor contract, with roll schedule/identifier, on every day needed for 756-observation warm-up and holding-period returns.
- Do not use FRED/EIA spot prices, raw front-contract C1 differences, broad baskets (DBC/DBA), leveraged daily-reset products, or delisted iPath symbols with only stale/incomplete history as substitutes. The 2020 USO rule change is in the existing market and must remain disclosed, not “corrected” by picking a favorable alternate.
