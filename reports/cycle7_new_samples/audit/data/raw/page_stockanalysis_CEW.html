About CEW

Fund Home Page

The WisdomTree Emerging Currency Strategy Fund (CEW) is an exchange-traded fund that mostly invests in long emerging markets basket, short usd currency. The fund tracks the change of money market rates and value of currencies of select emerging market countries relative to the US dollar. CEW was launched on May 6, 2009 and is issued by WisdomTree.
