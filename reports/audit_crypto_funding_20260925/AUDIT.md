# Independent Binance funding development audit — 2026-09-25

Scope: Binance USD-M perpetuals, UTC 2020-01-01 through 2022-12-31 only. No 2023–2026 archive file was opened. The frozen implementation and saved result were read only after the independent rebuild, for specification and comparison. No active Cycle 5 path was modified.

## Result and provenance

The frozen run is mechanically reproducible **as of the source archive available when its result was written**. The rebuild selects exactly the same long and short contracts and weights on all 792 traded days. It independently obtains the original 21-day, 5,000-draw bootstrap p = **0.0383923**. The only daily return mismatch is **2022-12-31**, by +0.000114134 in the original output, attributable to its `(t, t+1 day]` funding convention including the 2023-01-01 00:00 settlement. This inference uses only the difference between two development daily series; the locked settlement itself was not read. Excluding that out-of-window record yields base Sharpe 1.65447, annualized arithmetic mean 46.2553%, stress mean 26.8869%, and max drawdown -25.7638%. The saved report says 1.65466, 46.2605%, 26.8922%, and -25.7638%. The bootstrap p is unchanged at the reported precision.

The source data were not frozen with the result. The original result file was written at 04:15:12 local time. Of the 7,893 currently present development ZIPs, 1,598 were written after that time (at approximately 08:xx). The as-of rebuild has 145 contracts with development klines, while the current archive has 160: AAVE, KSM, LINA, LINK, MKR, NEO, OCEAN, ONT, SKL, UNFI, WAVES, XLM, XMR, ZEC, ZIL (all USDT). ## Construction and data checks

- As-of universe: 145 USDT perps with 2020–2022 klines after the four stated exclusions; 25–100 eligible contracts per traded day (mean 82.98), top-ranked by median prior-day quote volume over the preceding 30 calendar days. The rebuilt per-day universe and both quintile legs are in the parquet files. The implemented rolling median allows 15 of 30 observations; 1,655 of 65,720 selected universe-asset-days have only 15–29 prior volume observations. The plan did not explicitly state this minimum, so it should be documented as an implementation choice, not silently interpreted as a full 30-day requirement.
- Funding signal is the sum of settlements in `[t-7 days,t)` divided by seven, with at least ten settlements. The kline opens are all at 00:00 UTC. Funding `calc_time` is overwhelmingly 00:00, 08:00 and 16:00 UTC, with a small number of other even-hour settlements. A 00:00 settlement is excluded from the opening-day cash flow and included in the previous day's `(t,t+1 day]` flow. There are **no spot prices or spot timestamps** in this experiment; price P&L uses the same-day perpetual-futures open and close.
- Within the daily top 100, exact funding ties use lexical symbol order, not liquidity order. `floor(20% × universe size)` contracts are long at +0.5 gross and the same count short at -0.5 gross. The rebuilt book has zero net dollar weight and gross weight 1 on every traded day. Daily nominal target weights are compared directly with the previous day's target; there is no post-return drift correction. The price leg is `weight × (close/open - 1)`; funding is `-weight × settlement rate`. Base fees are 7 bp times summed absolute weight changes (about 7.00 percentage points/year as-of); stress fees are 20 bp (about 20.00 points/year). As-of price and funding contributions are +30.6747 and +22.5813 points/year, respectively, before fees.
- Delisted/dead contracts are present: LUNA development klines end 2022-05-13; FTT and SRM have development klines through 2022-12-31. A missing held day is assigned zero price return in gross/base, and a 20% adverse move times absolute position in stress. Of 27 as-of held missing contract-days, only 8 have no later development kline, while **19 are temporary gaps**. Thus the stress column is a missing-data shock, not a clean delisting loss. The stress missing-kline drag is about 6.37 points/year as-of. The base zero-return rule can also misstate actual liquidation outcomes. No executable delisting exit price is reconstructed from this dataset.
- Daily NAV is compounded from the daily open-to-close plus settlement/funding and fee returns. Missing-universe days are omitted from the NAV series; the as-of series has 304 such early days. The current archive has 227. No 2023–2026 window was used for warmup, signal, or returns in this audit.

## Null and factor audit

The reported p is a correctly recomputed one-sided p for a **mean-zero, centered circular block bootstrap** of the aggregate daily net-return series. Resampling the *portfolio* day preserves same-day cross-contract dependence and market-wide shocks already embedded in that day's P&L. It does not create independent contract samples. Its 21-day blocks partially preserve funding/return autocorrelation and repeated-contract effects, but break longer episodes and assume one stationary return law despite expansion from 25 to 100 eligible contracts, 2021 concentration and terminal/missing-data events. The one-sided p changes materially with block length in the as-of sample: 7 days 0.0122; 14 days 0.0256; 21 days 0.0384; 42 days 0.0540; 63 days 0.0622; 90 days 0.0776. The saved `P(pass|null)=0.011` is also not the same 5,000-draw bootstrap: its implementation uses 1,000 outer draws and a 200-draw nested p test. It does not repair the null's regime assumptions. Thus p=0.038 is computationally reproducible but **not trustworthy as stand-alone evidence of a stable or deployable edge**; the registered result failed its 0.025 gate.

As-of contemporaneous diagnostic betas (21-day HAC t-statistics where applicable): BTC-only -0.0031, ETH-only -0.0044, true equal-weight daily top-100 +0.0076. The joint model with BTC, ETH, market, liquidity, volatility and momentum high-minus-low return diagnostics has R² 0.201. Its volatility high-minus-low beta is **+0.2124 (HAC t=2.90)**; liquidity beta -0.0303 (t=-0.80), momentum beta -0.0384 (t=-1.59). The position book is modestly tilted toward higher quote liquidity: mean log-quote-volume long minus short +0.225. It is dollar-neutral and nearly market-beta-neutral over this sample, but not volatility-factor neutral. Actual market capitalization/size data are not present; quote liquidity is only a proxy and no true cap-size beta can be claimed. The original report's “equal-weight universe” beta is actually computed from **held long/short quintile contracts only**, not the registered top-100 benchmark; the audit calculates both. Factors are descriptive diagnostics and did not change the frozen weights.

## Concentration and interpretation

As-of arithmetic base-return sums: 2020 (62 traded days) +15.63%, 2021 +90.34%, 2022 **-5.61%**. Compounded 2022 is -6.77%; stress 2022 arithmetic sum is -31.55%. The 2021 sum is about 90% of the three-year traded-day sum. January 2021 alone contributes +43.51%, and the best three months together +79.95% versus the total +100.37% arithmetic sum. The top five positive asset attributions (CRV, DOGE, BEL, SOL, TRB) sum to about 71% of total net P&L, offset by losses elsewhere. The current archive reduces but does not remove regime concentration: 2021 +90.17% of a +125.20% total; 2022 remains negative at -2.11% arithmetic. External-exchange replication is scientifically justified **only as a new, separately registered falsification/replication of the exact frozen rule**, with complete point-in-time listings/delistings, settlement timing, exit-price treatment and precommitted cost/null analysis. It is not a backdoor validation or an upgrade of the failed registered Binance result.
