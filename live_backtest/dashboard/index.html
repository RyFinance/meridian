20)TL;DR

        

          
Post-earnings drift. After a company beats or misses analyst estimates, its stock tends to keep moving the same way for weeks. Meridian scores each fresh report by standardized surprise (SUE), turns that into a calibrated probability of drift, sizes it with fractional Kelly, and holds for 20 trading days.

          
Honest numbers. Early sweeps showed Sharpe ≈ 1.4 on a cash basis with a zero risk-free rate; marked daily against T-bills it is ≈ 0.7. Three pre-registered hold-outs did not confirm an edge, so the strategy now earns its place on a forward test that runs on data that did not exist when it was built.

40)WHAT MERIDIAN DOESGitHub ↗

        

          
After every US market close Meridian reads which of 75 large stocks just reported earnings, scores how much each one beat or missed estimates, and buys (or shorts) the ones the model expects to keep drifting. Each position is sized by the risk gate and sold after 20 trading days. You pick where the orders go:

          

            
SIMULATORNo keys. Real prices, simulated fills. Starts at $100,000.

            
READ-ONLYYour broker account shows next to the simulator. Nothing is sent.

            
BROKER PAPERPaper / sandbox keys. Real broker fills on fake money.

            
LIVEOpt-in. Real market orders on your account, capped per order.

44)HOW TO USE IT PROPERLY

        

          
    
            
  1. Start on the simulator and let it run a few weeks. Watch fills, signals and the equity curve, not one day's P&L.
    2. 
            
  2. Move to broker paper. Same orders, real broker fills. Check that the FILLS pane matches your broker's paper account.
    3. 
            
  3. Go live small. Set the capital that sizes orders to what you can lose, keep the per-order cap low, raise it slowly.
    4. 
            
  4. Check the desk after each close: heartbeat fresh, errors none, kill switch clear, scheduler RUNNING.
    5. 
            
  5. Stop instantly with Engage kill switch in SYSTEM (or switch to SIMULATOR in F2). Scheduled exits keep running so positions still close.
    6. 
            
  6. Don't trade the same account by hand in the same names. The bot only knows its own ledger.
    7. 
          

        

      

      
45)HOW THE NUMBERS STAY HONEST

        

          
    
            
  • Real prices. Simulated fills use the real last price; broker modes record the broker's own fill price and order id.
    • 
            
  • Real costs. Every round trip is charged 10 bps, the same as every backtest in the repo.
    • 
            
  • Marked daily. Sharpe and drawdown use positions at each day's close against T-bill returns, not cash-basis NAV.
    • 
            
  • Hard limits. 5% per position, 100% total exposure, kill switch at a 20% drawdown, 20-day holds.
    • 
            
  • Gate first. No order without a single-use approval from the risk gate, in every mode.
    •
