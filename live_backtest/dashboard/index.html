20)TL;DR

        

          
Post-earnings drift. After a company beats or misses analyst estimates, its stock tends to keep moving the same way for weeks. Meridian scores each fresh report by standardized surprise (SUE), turns that into a calibrated probability of drift, sizes it with fractional Kelly, and holds for 20 trading days.

          
Honest numbers. Early sweeps showed Sharpe ≈ 1.4 on a cash basis with a zero risk-free rate; marked daily against T-bills it is ≈ 0.7. Three pre-registered hold-outs did not confirm an edge, so the strategy now earns its place on a forward test that runs on data that did not exist when it was built.

31)HOW A TRADE HAPPENS

        

          
    
            
  1. After the close, the cycle reads which of the 75 watched names just reported.
    2. 
            
  2. Each report gets a surprise score and a calibrated probability of drift.
    3. 
            
  3. The risk gate decides: kill switch, drawdown cap, circuit breaker, exposure and Kelly size. It is the only way to an order.
    4. 
            
  4. Approved orders fill in the mode you chose: simulator, broker paper, or live.
    5. 
            
  5. Positions exit on schedule after 20 trading days. Exits are never blocked.
    6. 
          

        

      

      
32)SAFETY

        

          
    
            
  • Simulator by default. Nothing reaches a broker until you link one and pick a mode.
    • 
            
  • Live is opt-in three ways. MERIDIAN_ALLOW_LIVE=yes in .env, live credentials, and the arm phrase typed here. Disarming takes effect before the next order.
    • 
            
  • Capped. Each live order is clipped to MERIDIAN_LIVE_MAX_ORDER_USD.
    • 
            
  • Local only. The desk binds to 127.0.0.1. Credentials stay in bot/state (mode 600, git-ignored).
