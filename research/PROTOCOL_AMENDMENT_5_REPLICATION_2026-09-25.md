# Protocol Amendment 5 — Replication of Documented Premia

Status: **FINAL — awaiting owner registration; not registered and not an instruction to run returns.** Applies prospectively to the Core Premia Book only after the owner registers its hash and each component's code/data hashes. It does not reopen any discovery trial or change an old verdict. Amendment 4 numbering collided with another document; that other document remains untouched and unapproved. All implementation-model specifications must cite **Amendment 5** for this replication protocol.

## A. Paper-locked specification and provenance

The registered components are **1: MOP trend** and **2: KMVP carry**, then a separately registered combination. A component plan must transcribe each source rule, cite a page/section, identify paper vintage and earliest public circulation, list all deviations, and freeze source code, instrument-level data and external benchmark before its first return. No own return, Sharpe, drawdown or subperiod may choose a parameter, market, vendor, start date, cost or fix. Hurst's 1/3/12-month blend is corroborating published evidence, **not an additional variant in component 1**; testing it would require a separately registered component.

| Component | Paper rule that must be implemented or explicitly marked unavailable |
|---|---|
| MOP trend | [Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen (2012), §2.4 p.233, §3.2 pp.233–234 and Eq. 5 p.236](https://fairmodel.econ.yale.edu/ec439/mosk.pdf): end-of-month sign of preceding 12-month **excess** return, long if positive/short if negative, one-month holding and monthly rebalance; each instrument position is `sign × 40%/lagged annualized volatility`, then equal average across available instruments. Their volatility is an EW mean-centred squared *daily excess-return* estimator, annualized by 261, with decay `δ/(1−δ)=60` trading days (`δ=60/61`); estimate available at `t−1` is applied to `t`. Represent all four classes (equity, government bond, commodity, currency) with the mechanically selected free-data universe below; the paper's 58 instruments are a reference inventory, **not** a mandatory acquisition target. The [AQR TSMOM monthly series](https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Datasets/Time-Series-Momentum-Factors-Monthly) is the independent primary fidelity arbiter, not a net executable return target. |
| Hurst corroboration (not component 1's signal) | [Hurst, Ooi & Pedersen (2017), “Constructing the Time-Series Momentum Strategy,” journal p.16, Appendices A–B](https://fairmodel.econ.yale.edu/ec439/hurst.pdf): documents a different equal 1-, 3- and 12-month trend blend, monthly rebalance across 67 markets, equal ex-ante-vol positions and 10% ex-ante portfolio target. It informs breadth and cost-realism requirements; its different signal is not silently compared with MOP's AQR factor or included in the frozen trend component. |
| Component 2 — KMVP carry | [Koijen, Moskowitz, Pedersen & Vrugt (2018), §2 pp.200–204, §3.2–3.4 pp.207–210, Eqs. 19–20 and Table 2 pp.208–209](https://spinup-000d1a-wp-offload-media.s3.amazonaws.com/faculty/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2019/04/Carry.pdf): carry is the pre-known return if spot price is unchanged, using the asset-class-specific spot/forward, yield or curve formula in §2; use **current end-month carry (`carry1m`)**, not the alternative 12-month smoother. Within each available asset class, rank all eligible securities; `w_i,t=z_t[rank(C_i,t)−(N_t+1)/2]`, normalized so long weights sum to +1 and short weights to −1; next-month return is `Σ_i w_i,t r_i,t+1`; monthly rebalance. Paper §3.4 combines asset-class portfolios by equal-vol weighting using *in-sample* volatility. **Declared executable deviation:** at month-end `t`, each class with at least 36 prior monthly carry-portfolio returns through `t` is scaled by `0.10/σ_class,t`, where `σ_class,t=√12·sd(returns through t)` is expanding-window, and eligible scaled classes are equally averaged for `t+1`. Exclude classes still in warm-up, never use future returns, and record this deviation in component 2's prereg. Do not add or omit a class based on realized performance. |

For every component, the plan fixes contract continuity/roll and return adjustment; signal close, release and execution timestamps; tie and zero handling; missingness and eligibility; volatility floor/warm-up; class aggregation; currency/collateral/financing; spreads, commissions, roll, impact, borrow and stress costs. A choice absent from the papers is a **researcher degree of freedom**: put its exact value, rationale and alternative-not-tested flag in the registered plan. Paper returns may be calculated only through a snapshot-verified registered command.

**Free-data deviation and fixed floor.** A component may use a reduced universe and standard synthetic excess-return constructions, all declared by market before returns: equity total return minus cash; constant-maturity government bonds from lagged yield/duration/convexity plus carry; FX spot return plus contemporaneously known short-rate differential; and commodity ETF total return minus cash (with embedded fund expenses and roll exposure). Freeze the exact formulas, rates, source and investable proxy in the component registration. Never call these identical to futures; the external AQR test arbitrates trend fidelity. The trend universe must have **≥8 distinct markets**, **≥1 in each of four classes**, and **≥120 identical monthly dates** on which every included class portfolio and its AQR class/overall benchmark are defined. A market counts once, not once per share class or synthetic alias. The 120-month floor is power-derived: Fisher-z one-sided α=`0.05/5=0.01` across overall plus four class correlations needs effective `n≈45` to distinguish true `ρ=.92` from `.80` overall and `n≈47` for `.85` from `.65` by class at 80% power. These are **assumed design alternatives**, not observed correlations; lower true fidelity has lower power. A conservative assumed monthly design effect `D=2.4` leaves `120/2.4=50` effective months. This powers only the correlation leg, not beta, tracking error or the full joint gate; actual effective sample size and calibrated inference follow Amendment 4. Eight markets are a minimal representation check, **not** a time-series power multiplier. Below any floor: **FAIL before returns**, not a search for replacements. Above the floor but failing preregistered fidelity: **FAIL**. No rescue by changing market composition, construction or threshold.

## B. Fidelity-only corrections

Two immutable series are retained: a **gross paper-fidelity shadow** aligned to the relevant published benchmark/statistic (same dates, currency, excess-return and risk scale), and the **net implementable** series with attainable execution and financing. Diagnose the former against the independent source; estimate economic performance with the latter. Never tune to the latter's Sharpe.

A legitimate fix corrects a demonstrable transcription, timestamp/look-ahead, unit, currency, corporate-action, contract-roll, missing-file or cashflow bug *relative to the frozen paper rule and independently sourced records*. Before any rerun, append a dated issue with evidence, affected records, before/after code and manifest hashes, predicted mechanical direction and a new dataset/code version. Preserve the original output and publish both. If a fix alters signal, lookback, weighting, universe eligibility, cost assumption, benchmark mapping or a paper-unspecified default, it is a **new registered implementation**, not a bug fix. It may not replace the original on the strength of better returns or fidelity. AQR is a calibration reference, not an instruction to fit a factor.

## C. Replication acceptance; no counterfeit holdout

**Component 1 — trend primary gate.** Before returns, map the gross MOP series to the [AQR Time Series Momentum monthly factors](https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Datasets/Time-Series-Momentum-Factors-Monthly) overall and in each of the four asset classes, with currency, excess-return definition, gross/net basis, target scale and overlapping months documented. On identical monthly dates report `n`, Pearson `ρ`, OLS-with-intercept `β=Cov(rep,ref)/Var(ref)`, annual tracking error `TE=√12·sd(rep−ref)`, and `TE/σ_ref` with `σ_ref=√12·sd(ref)`. No return-fitted rescaling. **Retained preregistered thresholds:** overall `n≥120`, `ρ≥0.80`, `0.80≤β≤1.20`, `TE/σ_ref≤0.75`; each class `n≥60`, `ρ≥0.65`, `0.70≤β≤1.30`, `TE/σ_ref≤0.90`. Every pair must pass, along with acquisition completeness. Bands permit different data vintages, contract coverage and rolls while demanding strong matched movement and scale; they cannot be loosened after returns.

**Component 2 — carry two-tier gate.** Carry may also use the declared reduced universe and synthetic constructions under the fixed four-class/eight-market/120-month floor; absence of KMVP's other five classes is not an automatic pre-return stop. Tier **(a), primary and binding:** reproduce the gross *Carry1M* row in KMVP Table 2 Panel A for **each mechanically selected class**, including at least global equities, global government bonds, currencies and commodities, over that class's published sample bounds, with at least 95% of its published monthly observations and no unexplained gaps. Compare annualized excess mean, annualized standard deviation and annualized Sharpe using the paper's annualization and class-specific return normalization. For every selected class require `|mean_rep−mean_paper|≤max(2 percentage points, 0.25·|mean_paper|)`, `|vol_rep−vol_paper|≤max(2 percentage points, 0.20·vol_paper)`, and `|Sharpe_rep−Sharpe_paper|≤0.30`. The fixed absolute floor accommodates low-return/low-vol classes; proportional bands accommodate very different scales, vendor vintages and proxy rolls; the Sharpe bound prevents scale alone from passing. All three metrics for every selected class must pass. Missing paper-period coverage, an uncomparable selected class, or an out-of-band metric is **FAIL**, not a discretionary omission. The executable expanding-vol deviation affects *aggregation only*, not these class-level comparisons. Tier **(b), secondary diagnostic:** correlate the gross global carry series with the frozen [AQR Century carry](https://www.aqr.com/insights/datasets/century-of-factor-premia-monthly) column on ≥120 common months; report `ρ`, `β` and `TE/σ_ref`, and flag `ρ<0.35`. The lower `0.35` threshold is deliberate because the AQR Century factor belongs to a different paper, universe and construction; this checks broad common exposure, **not** exact fidelity. Tier (b) cannot pass or rescue tier (a), and its failure is disclosed but is not a veto. The owner's earlier carry decision keeps published-summary tier (a), **not AQR**, as carry's binding arbiter; the AQR primary arbiter applies to trend. Carry enters the combined book **only if tier (a) passes**; otherwise omit it under the predeclared two-sleeve fallback, with no construction changes. Published summary-statistic fidelity is coarse in-sample reconstruction evidence, **not** a post-publication performance claim.

Report block-bootstrap uncertainty for trend fidelity and carry's secondary correlation with a predeclared 12-month mean stationary block and 10,000 seeded draws; point-estimate thresholds remain the fixed gates. Published carry summaries are deterministic targets, not p-values. Differences outside bands are disclosed as failures, never repaired by optimizing our own performance.

For each component, use the **earliest evidenced public circulation**, not later journal publication, as the cutoff; first eligible full month is the next month. A 2010 MOP working paper is cited in contemporaneous literature (the journal version became available online 11 Dec 2011); KMVP circulated as [NBER WP 19325 in Aug 2013](https://www.nber.org/papers/w19325). Conservative fixed dates when an exact earlier day is unproven: **MOP 2011-01-01**, **KMVP 2013-09-01**. [Hurst's Fall 2012 white paper](https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Bibliography/Trend-Following) is contextual evidence, not component 1's cutoff. Report the frozen net implementation after each cutoff: annualized excess mean, volatility, Sharpe, CAGR/total-return convention, maximum drawdown, costs, market betas, HAC or time-block confidence intervals, and instrument/class coverage. This is the **live-expectation estimate**, not a development or validation gate; negative outcomes stand. Famous published strategies have no clean historical holdout—their paths and design choices are public. Never call post-publication history an untouched holdout, and do not rescue it by revising the rule.

## D. Controls and Amendment-2 guards

Run on identical dates, eligible instruments, volatility scaling, execution and applicable costs: trend against an **always-long** portfolio of the same instruments (exposure matched); carry against pre-seeded **random-rank** long–short books with identical class/date/rank-weight construction. Also measure each component and control against equity and government-bond excess-return betas, using frozen external equity/bond series. Paired time-block inference preserves common months; no asset-by-asset independent resampling. These controls diagnose the economic mechanism; they do not replace the fidelity gate.

| Guard | Replication mapping |
|---|---|
| G1 beta | Report pre/post-publication equity/bond and same-class market betas, HAC intercept and alpha share versus net mean. Retain the `t≥2`, `≥50%` **promotion diagnostic** where estimable, not a fidelity test of a published premium. |
| G2 reduced exposure | Paired comparison with the always-long or random-rank control and with a vol-matched control; retain `t≥2` as a promotion diagnostic. A carry placebo distribution must keep the changing cross-section fixed. |
| G3 concentration | Retain top-5%-months `<50%` of P&L, max-year `<35%`, leave-one-year-out Sharpe `≥0.5×` full, and sign-test `p<.05`; also show best contiguous 12/24 months removed. Interpret pre- and post-publication separately. |
| G4 one asset | Retain largest-asset `<50%` of P&L and positive leave-one-asset-out Sharpe where decomposable; show class and market contribution. Single-market exposure is flagged, not disguised as diversified evidence. |
| G5 null calibration | For any inferential *promotion* claim, use a mean-zero time-block null and estimate the false-pass probability of the **entire registered promotion rule** (`≤.05`), preserving cross-market dependence and universe history. Fidelity is not a zero-alpha null and must not be shoehorned into this test. |
| G6 post-publication | Retain the `t≥1.65` post-publication diagnostic if ≥60 months, but it is **not clean holdout certification**. Report even if it fails; do not tune/rescue. |
| G7 execution lag | Run the predeclared next-tradable-session lag and report Sharpe retention versus the original (`≥60%` promotion diagnostic), including roll and financing. |

Amendment-2 G1–G7 cannot by themselves authorize capital here. The discovery validation-pass prerequisite has no historical analogue for literature replication; a future capital standard needs separate user approval and truly prospective evidence.

## E. Combined Core Premia Book (separate registration)

Combine **frozen** trend, carry and a fixed equity/bond core only after each sleeve's construction, cost model, data manifest and acceptance rule are registered. Carry is included **iff** its tier-(a) gate passes; if it fails or is indeterminate, use the already specified **trend + core** two-sleeve book. If trend fails its primary gate, no Core Premia Book is evaluated. The core is **60% SPY / 40% IEF**, dividends reinvested, monthly rebalanced; these USD ETFs are a fixed investable equity/bond default for the post-publication book, not a backfilled pre-inception proxy. ETF expenses are embedded in total returns. At each month-end estimate the trailing **60 monthly net excess-return** covariance of the eligible two or three sleeves using only earlier months, shrink it 25% toward its diagonal, and solve nonnegative weights summing to one with equal ex-ante risk contributions (`w_i(Σw)_i` equal). If fewer than 60 common observations or covariance/solver failure, use normalized `1/σ_i` weights from lagged individual volatilities; if those are unavailable, remain in cash. No expected-return input and no weights chosen from realized combined Sharpe. Rebalance monthly at next tradable execution, then scale the aggregate to **10% annual ex-ante volatility** using the same lagged covariance, subject to a fixed **2× gross-notional/NAV cap**. Cash earns max(0, 3-month U.S. T-bill yield minus 0.50 percentage points); financed exposure pays the 3-month U.S. T-bill yield plus 2 percentage points, with actual instrument margin/borrow charges used if higher. Record achieved risk, leverage, collateral and financing; no risk-target retrofits. The core, estimator, cash rates, cap and fallback are **researcher defaults**, not claims from the papers, frozen here before returns.

After freezing, report combined net performance and a **non-design** 12% annual total-return scenario: use the lower of the post-publication annualized net arithmetic mean and its predeclared one-sided 75% time-block lower confidence bound; solve for constant leverage under the above financing rule, observed turnover and capacity costs. If that conservative mean is nonpositive or required leverage exceeds 2×, report “not attainable,” not an alternative book. From the same frozen monthly net history, report median/5th/95th percentile maximum drawdown under **10,000**, seed `20260925`, 10-year paired stationary-block paths (mean block **12 months**), preserving sleeve covariance; state estimation and regime uncertainty. The 12% goal never feeds back into selection, sizing or target volatility.

## F. Power and paper-only forward rule

Before returns, disclose precision for trend's **fidelity correlations** and carry's **published-summary differences** on their effective months (autocorrelation-adjusted; high-power mechanical questions when overlap is long), separately from the minimum detectable net mean/Sharpe and interval width for **post-publication performance** (often low-power). Asset count never multiplies effective months. The discovery development/validation thresholds and power verdicts do not apply. Report block length, false-fidelity placebo calibration, actual `n_eff`, interval coverage and multiplicity across registered component/class comparisons. If an inferential claim is made, predeclare and simulate its test size; do not assume a short monthly block bootstrap is calibrated. An inconclusive performance interval is not a failed replication of mechanics.

**Paper-only forward observation may start** after (i) the registered, verified snapshot and acquisition-completeness review; (ii) component-1 fidelity pass and reconciled accounting, with carry included only upon its tier-(a) pass; (iii) full post-publication, cost, control, guard, concentration and power disclosures; (iv) frozen conditional two-/three-sleeve rule and independent code/data audit; and (v) a prospective, externally anchored forward rule registered *before* the first observation. It is observation only: **no capital/account permissions, paid-data authorization or orders are granted**. The user alone could decide any later capital deployment under a separately approved standard.

**Inference dependency.** All fidelity-test and post-publication inference must use the calibrated size, block/bootstrap, effective-sample and multiplicity procedures of **Protocol Amendment 4** once that amendment is finalized by its separate owner. This document does not define, revise or approve those procedures. **Amendment 5 cannot be registered before Amendment 4 is finalized and registered.** If Amendment 4 requires a stricter inference implementation, use it without altering the frozen signal, coverage floor or descriptive fidelity thresholds; a substantive conflict returns to the owner before any returns.

## G. Data integrity and completeness

Every input, generated table, dynamic universe, exclusion, mapping, delisting and benchmark (including AQR and Ken French if used) is frozen under a versioned `research.data_snapshot` manifest before the first component or combined return. Register the command with `{snapshot_root}` and `{membership_path}`; use `run` so live-file, manifest, membership and content hashes are verified **before** evaluation. A changed/missing/new historical file requires a new dataset version; replay of the old frozen view is labelled replay. No evaluator may read around the frozen view.

Attach a pre-return acquisition-completeness record for **each endpoint**: URL/API and retrieval UTC; expected versus received instrument count and date range by class; missing/delisted instruments and intervals; failed downloads and retries with final status; row/time bounds and duplicate checks; contract-roll and futures adjustment method (including price vs return adjustment and cashflows); currency, timezone, holiday and collateral conventions; source version; snapshot manifest hash. Compare each candidate against the historical inventory; an unexplained gap fails that candidate's fixed quality filter, and a resulting coverage-floor miss is **FAIL**, not a discretionary replacement. External factor files are revised, so their bytes, vintage, definition sheets and retrieval time are frozen too. A hash proves identity, **not completeness or economic correctness**.
