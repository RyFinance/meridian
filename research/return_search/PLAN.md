# Broad return search — frozen design, 19 September 2026

Objective: highest net compound return among specified candidates, with no leverage or shorting. All trials remain recorded. Parameters will not be swept. This is a new selection protocol on a previously researched historical database, not a claim that these dates are globally untouched.

Training: expanding annual training, starting 2008 where available; labels must finish before each prediction year. Prediction/selection: 2019-01-01 through 2025-06-30. Evaluation: 2025-07-01 through 2026-09-17, accessible only after a signed selection file. Model hyperparameters and features fixed below. The evaluation is run once for selected candidates and fixed benchmarks, without changing the selection after seeing it.

Fields: price returns 5/21/63/126/252 sessions, skipped-month momentum, volatility 21/63, downside volatility, proximity to 252-session high, distance to 200-session average, intraday return, overnight return, volume ratio; enriched versions also use VIX, VIX/VIX3M, long/short interest-rate slope, credit ETF relative trend, market trend, aggregate option call-volume share and options volume surprise, with missing-data indicators. Macro is observed financial-market data lagged one session; revised CPI/GDP are excluded. Options are predictors of stock returns, not fictitious executable option quotes. Insider data lacks adequate history. Existing COT and option-writing trials remain in the prior research ledger; no retroactive relabeling of them as new trials.

Universes: available current 503-stock cache (explicit survivorship bias; excluded from qualified primary selection); existing ETF cache covering global equities, bonds, credit, currencies, commodities and real estate; BTC/ETH continuous daily Yahoo bars; SVXY for actual volatility ETF returns. No failed/missing asset prices may be silently forward-filled in execution. SVXY leverage changed historically, so its observed fund returns, not a synthetic fixed exposure, are used.

Candidates (20): stock and ETF each have 7: 12-1 momentum, multihorizon risk-adjusted trend, breakout, price-feature ridge, price-feature gradient boost, enriched ridge, enriched gradient boost. Crypto has 4: multihorizon trend, breakout, price ridge, price gradient boost. Volatility has 2: contango gate, contango plus SPY trend. Four passive same-universe controls are outside candidate selection.

Ridge: StandardScaler, alpha 100. Gradient boost: 100 iterations, 7 leaves, learning rate .05, min 100 samples per leaf, l2=10, early_stopping=False, seed 20260919. Training on weekly observations avoids heavily repeated labels; stock training pool includes at most prior-ADV top 200 names with >=252 bars and ADV >=$20m. Return label: next-executable-open to open 21 sessions later, or 30 days for crypto. Clip training returns +/-50%. No per-symbol ID feature. Missing values filled with zero after adding missingness indicators. Training starts after all main price features exist.

Portfolios: monthly stock/ETF decisions at first session close, weekly Monday crypto decisions, daily volatility decisions. Equal weight among top 20 stocks or top 4 ETFs with positive signal; crypto holds BTC/ETH in equal slots whenever their signal is positive. Unused slots stay cash. Equity/ETF/volatility execute next open; crypto waits two full date rows (one complete intervening UTC day) to avoid the close/open boundary issue. Max gross 100%; no leverage, no borrowing. Cash earns lagged IRX/100 pro rata over elapsed days, without future rate backfill. Base one-way costs: stocks/ETFs 5bps; SVXY 10bps; crypto 20bps. Stress: 15/30/50bps respectively. Final liquidation charged. Equity dividends/fund expenses are embedded in adjusted bars.

Selection: among the 20 candidates maximize selection-period net CAGR, subject to annual volatility <=40%, maximum drawdown <=50%, positive stress excess mean, and positive total return in at least 4 of the 6 full years 2019–2024. Record unconstrained leaders too. Select one winner per family and the best overall non-stock candidate; stock winner is exploratory due to survivorship. Freeze one additional equal-weight blend of eligible non-stock family winners. This blend has no optimized weights. Evaluation runs only frozen winners, the blend, and passive controls. Annual refitting during evaluation is permitted only with matured past labels, per the fixed rule.

Report CAGR, total return, excess Sharpe, annual volatility, max drawdown, worst calendar year, turnover, base/stress costs, and chronological fold results. Compare final selected model against passive controls and report bootstrap uncertainty, without changing model choice or tuning afterwards. A 14.5-month evaluation is not evidence of a durable undiscovered edge by itself.
