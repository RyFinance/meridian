# Independent mechanical audit — frozen M1

**Scope.** This audit used only the declared `agent_mnav` authority set (frozen rules/amendment, development and sealed artifacts, cached SEC sources, `mnav_core.py`, `dev_research.py`, `validate_frozen.py`, and the final report where relevant).  It expressly excludes the unexpected concurrent holdout/forward files.  The repository graph service was unavailable, so this is targeted raw-file inspection.  All new artifacts are confined to this `agent_mnav_audit` directory.

## Conclusion

The original M1 result is **mechanically reproducible**, but it is **not strictly causal under its stated 16:00 ET cutoff**.  `mnav_core.py` includes a one-minute BTC candle stamped 16:00 (`searchsorted(..., side="right")`), while the source convention labels a candle by its start: its close is available at 16:01.  This is a one-minute future-data use, not a parameter-search or accounting error.

The isolated completed-bar rerun is the record suitable for a causal M1 result: **+532.0078% total return, 66.8806% CAGR, 1.14642 Sharpe, -46.9672% maximum drawdown, 36 round trips**.  It holds the frozen M1 rule, SEC inputs, MSTR inputs, split adjustment, next-open simulator, and 3 bp + $0.005/actual-share costs fixed.  It differs from the reported +534.2088% / 1.14837 Sharpe by **-2.2011 percentage points total return** and **-0.001947 Sharpe**, with unchanged drawdown.  No other severe mechanical defect was found.

## Checks that passed

**SEC availability and disclosures.** The parser accepts only BTC "as of" dates no later than the filing date ([`mnav_core.py:51-105`](/Users/rytty/CCP/quant_v7/research/mstr_btc_0dte/agent_mnav/mnav_core.py:51)), then makes a filing usable only at the close of the first strictly later MSTR session ([`mnav_core.py:165-178`](/Users/rytty/CCP/quant_v7/research/mstr_btc_0dte/agent_mnav/mnav_core.py:165)).  I checked all 24 parsed observations: every share/BTC as-of date is at or before its stated filing date and every activation date is strictly later.  Since a close decision is then traded only at the following open ([`mnav_core.py:231-288`](/Users/rytty/CCP/quant_v7/research/mstr_btc_0dte/agent_mnav/mnav_core.py:231)), the dated filing rule is conservative even for after-close filings.  The 2024-08-06 10-Q, for example, was EDGAR-accepted at 16:06:30; it first influences the 2024-08-08 open, not that filing day.

**Split.** The amendment correctly supersedes the original trade-date wording: price-basis shares are multiplied by 10 according to the active observation’s `share_asof`, not the current session ([`FROZEN_RULES_AMENDMENT_01.json:2-10`](/Users/rytty/CCP/quant_v7/research/mstr_btc_0dte/agent_mnav/FROZEN_RULES_AMENDMENT_01.json:2); [`mnav_core.py:180-189`](/Users/rytty/CCP/quant_v7/research/mstr_btc_0dte/agent_mnav/mnav_core.py:180)).  This properly retains the 10× factor after 2024-08-08 until the post-split cover count becomes active; the provided split sanity check shows the avoided false ~90% discontinuity.  Pre-split pseudo-share commission division is also correct ([`mnav_core.py:241-255`](/Users/rytty/CCP/quant_v7/research/mstr_btc_0dte/agent_mnav/mnav_core.py:241)).

**BTC gaps.** Apart from the P1 candle completion defect, the gap policy is implemented: quotes over 15 minutes stale become unavailable and are never filled forward ([`mnav_core.py:131-140`](/Users/rytty/CCP/quant_v7/research/mstr_btc_0dte/agent_mnav/mnav_core.py:131)); sessions and MSTR open-to-open P&L remain present while the signal holds its prior stance ([`mnav_core.py:195-201`](/Users/rytty/CCP/quant_v7/research/mstr_btc_0dte/agent_mnav/mnav_core.py:195)).  The causal correction has the same 1,405 valid BTC-quote days as the frozen path.

**P&L and statistics.** Position decisions use the preceding close’s signal, trades apply adverse 3 bp plus the correct fixed share commission at the next open, and final liquidation is at the final included open ([`mnav_core.py:251-312`](/Users/rytty/CCP/quant_v7/research/mstr_btc_0dte/agent_mnav/mnav_core.py:251)).  Spot checks reconcile the first buy: 18.9000 × 1.0003 + 0.0005 = 18.9061696 ([`SEALED_TRADES_M1.csv:2`](/Users/rytty/CCP/quant_v7/research/mstr_btc_0dte/agent_mnav/SEALED_TRADES_M1.csv:2)).  M1 ends flat, so a forced final exit does not affect its headline.  The independent zero-cost vector check passes at 3.55e-15 ([`validate_frozen.py:348-376`](/Users/rytty/CCP/quant_v7/research/mstr_btc_0dte/agent_mnav/validate_frozen.py:348)).  The reported Sharpe and drawdown are correctly computed on the open-to-open marked portfolio ([`mnav_core.py:315-346`](/Users/rytty/CCP/quant_v7/research/mstr_btc_0dte/agent_mnav/mnav_core.py:315)); they are not intraday statistics.

**Reproduction.** Rebuilding the panel from the two SEC manifests matched the sealed 1,448-session panel and M1 signals exactly; the unmodified simulator matched the sealed M1 summary and all 74 recorded M1 trades (numerical tolerance below 6e-14).  [`reproduce_causal_1559.py`](/Users/rytty/CCP/quant_v7/research/mstr_btc_0dte/agent_mnav_audit/reproduce_causal_1559.py) first asserts that exact frozen reproduction and pinned hashes, then makes only the completed-bar cutoff change. Use the 15:59 completed-bar result as the causal M1 paper record; do not label the original 16:00-close headline strictly no-lookahead.  Before relying on the record operationally, pin a source schema stating candle timestamp semantics and preserve EDGAR acceptance times alongside the existing filing manifest.
