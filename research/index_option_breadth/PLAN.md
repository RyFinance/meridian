# Index-option put/call volume as a one-week equity-risk signal

2026-09-23. Register before computing any strategy or control return. One candidate. The OPRA per-contract daily **volume** archive was aggregated for source QA only; no option signal was paired with a later return before registration.

## Distinct information and mechanism

The ledger's stock-option H1–H4 and IV V1–V3 trials used per-stock option/stock volume, IV spread, skew or IV changes; its index-option trial wrote one-day puts. None tested the **market-wide put-versus-call trade-volume balance in SPY, QQQ and IWM** as information for future equity risk. This uses order-flow intensity, not option trade prices or the already-tested put-writing payoff. The hypothesis is directional: a week unusually rich in protective put volume signals funding/hedging pressure that persists into the following week, so favor IEF; a call-rich week favors SPY. OPRA prints do not identify buyer versus seller or open versus close, so this mechanism is uncertain and must be tested against price and volatility controls. Do not reverse it after a failure.

## Data and point-in-time rules

- Historical source: LSE vault `options_1d` OPRA contract-day OHLCV, archived at `data/lse/options_etf_1d/{SPY,QQQ,IWM}.parquet`. Live LSE MCP `options_flow` confirms recent records are trade prints with no bid/ask or open interest. The historical files contain 2014-06 through 2026-09, but only rows through 2019-12-31 are eligible for this development run. `ts` is normalized to 00:00 UTC for the **trade date**, not an executable midnight price. The weekly statistic is first available after the final option session of the week; execution is at the next week's first ETF open.
- Source QA before returns: 2014–2019 has 1,407 shared option dates. For each underlying there are no duplicate `(ts,osi)` contract-day rows, no nonpositive volume, only `C`/`P`, and all daily timestamps are 00:00 UTC. Aggregate positive contract volume only when calendar days to expiry are **7 through 30 inclusive**. Require all three ETFs to have a record on a date; sum put volumes and call volumes over all three, then by Monday–Sunday week. There are 292 source weeks, all but the last have 4–5 option days. Source hashes and derived daily/weekly hashes are in `data/lse/index_option_breadth/manifest.json`.
- Use ETF prices from existing `data/multiasset/etf/{SPY,IEF}.parquet`, yfinance `auto_adjust=True` official-session adjusted opens/closes. VIX control from `data/multiasset/vol/VIX.parquet`. The underlying ETFs do not have current-stock-universe survivorship, but options contract terms and trade-volume definitions may change. No source as-of vintage is available; an unusually strong result needs independent vendor/OPRA volume checks.

## Frozen weekly rule and controls

- Decision: first common SPY/IEF trading day of each Monday–Sunday week. Use the **immediately preceding completed week** of 7–30-day put/call volume, requiring at least three shared option dates and positive totals. Let `q = log(weekly puts / weekly calls)`. Compare q with the median of the **52 preceding completed weeks**, excluding the signal week, each with at least three shared option days. If q is above that median, allocate 100% IEF for the coming week; otherwise allocate 100% SPY. Exact equality favors IEF. If any coverage rule fails, hold 50% SPY / 50% IEF for that week. No threshold, DTE window or horizon search.
- Mark daily adjusted open-to-next-open ETF total returns. Rebalance at the weekly decision open; charge 3 bp per side per dollar traded, including initial entry (`0.0003×[|Δw_SPY|+|Δw_IEF|]`). Stress is 9 bp per side. Long-only, fully invested, no borrow or leverage. No return crosses from development into validation.
- `C0`: static exposure-matched SPY/IEF mix, SPY fraction equal to the real strategy's **development daily-weighted SPY allocation**, charged initial entry cost. Each shift-null world gets its own matched static fraction. Report fixed investable 50/50 as well. Gate on active net return versus C0, never absolute portfolio Sharpe.
- `C1`: same weekly rotation/fills/costs, but SPY if its adjusted close rose over the immediately preceding completed week, otherwise IEF. `C2`: same, but SPY if VIX's adjusted close **fell** during the prior completed week, otherwise IEF. Each uses only the prior two weekly closing observations known before entry; missing means 50/50. These controls test whether option volume adds information beyond recent equity price and observable volatility.
- `P1`: reverse the option-signal allocation (1−SPY weight), same fills/costs, placebo only. `P2`: 5,000 circular shifts of the weekly option allocation path by **at least 52 weeks**, seed 20260923, preserving exposure mix/persistence but breaking alignment with future returns. Use `research.null_lab.api.run_null` in development-only mode with an exactness test for the custom engine.

## Windows, gate and lock

- Source warm-up 2014-06 through 2015-06. **Development:** first ETF week beginning 2015-07-06 through the last ETF open of 2019-12. T = calendar years from 2015-07-06 through 2019-12-31 divided by 365.25. **Validation:** 2020-01 through 2026-09, locked; do not load 2020+ option volume or ETF returns now.
- Pass development only if base-cost daily C0-active excess Sharpe ≥ `1.8/sqrt(T)`, stress-cost C0-active annualized mean > 0, and base-cost mean return versus both C1 and C2 is positive. Apply this **full gate** to null worlds and report P(pass|null) ± Monte-Carlo SE. Report paired one-sided 21-day circular-block bootstrap p-values for C0/C1/C2 and best contiguous 12/24-month-removal diagnostics. They cannot rescue a failed gate.
- If development passes, freeze code and audit source completeness, contemporaneous publication, factor/beta exposure, concentration, volatility regimes, transaction costs and multiple testing before a separate validation unlock. If ever unlocked, validation needs C0-active net Sharpe ≥ 0.75, stress active annualized mean > 0, one-sided 21-day block-bootstrap p < 0.05 for active mean, and positive net mean versus C1 and C2.
- If development fails, record the null, leave validation locked, and do not reverse or tune put/call ratio, expiries, aggregation, thresholds, windows or execution.
