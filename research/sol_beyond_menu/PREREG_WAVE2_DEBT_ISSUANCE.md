# Wave-2 preregistration: JKP global debt-issuance theme

**LOCKED BEFORE RETURNS; research-grade, not directly tradable.** No owner/forward registration, broker permission, or live orders.

Source: Jensen, Kelly and Pedersen (2023), *Journal of Finance*, “Is There a Replication Crisis in Finance?” https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13249 ; author Global Factor Data Documentation pp. 8 and 38, https://jkpfactors-data.s3.amazonaws.com/documents/Documentation.pdf . The source constructs monthly factors from signed high-minus-low characteristic terciles using non-microcap breakpoints, capped value weights, at least five stocks per leg and a four-month accounting-data lag; themes equal-weight the signed constituent factors. The published “debt_issuance” theme bets against high debt growth/financial-liability expansion and related balance-sheet growth. Documentation Table 9 p. 38 maps growth in book debt to Lyandres–Sun–Zhang (2008) and financial liabilities to Richardson et al. (2005). Mechanism: corporate financing/investment expansion may anticipate lower marginal returns or reflect overinvestment; arbitrage and shorting frictions can delay repricing. This is the paper's author-constructed theme, not an invented signal.

Data: frozen `sol_wave2_jkp_v1`, JKP `world`/`all_themes`/monthly/`vw_cap` archive, column `name=debt_issuance` and its published `ret`/`direction` (already signed). Other regions are not selected on their returns. Require exactly one debt-issuance row per calendar month and no missing return. Dollar-neutral long-short zero-investment portfolio; the simultaneous +1/−1 broad-market control is net zero. Residual market/size/value betas are diagnostic, not optimized away.

Sample: 1988-01 through 2025-12. **Holdout locked now: 2016-01 through 2025-12** (120/456 months, 26.3%; more than five years). Development only: 1988-01 through 2015-12 (336 months). The evaluator must stop before parsing numeric returns after 2015-12. A missing/duplicate development month causes failure without moving the boundary.
