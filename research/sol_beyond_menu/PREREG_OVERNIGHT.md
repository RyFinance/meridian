# Preregistration: overnight equity versus intraday equity

Status: LOCKED BEFORE RETURNS. Research only; not owner-registered or a forward signal.

Source: Cooper, Cliff, and Gulen, *Return Differences between Trading and Non-Trading Hours: Like Night and Day* (2008), and Boyarchenko, Larsen, and Whelan, *The Overnight Drift*, RFS 2023. The former defines the close-to-next-open versus same-day open-to-close contrast; the latter attributes overnight return to liquidity-provider inventory risk, with subsequent 2021–25 evidence of decay. Source URLs: https://doi.org/10.2139/SSRN.1004081 ; https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhad020 ; https://libertystreeteconomics.newyorkfed.org/2026/07/the-disappearing-overnight-drift/ . Paper definition is the close/open split, not a technical signal. This test is a declared SPY ETF implementation rather than their index-futures portfolio.

Universe: one broad liquid U.S. equity ETF, SPY. Input dates 1993-01-29 through 2022-12-30. **Locked holdout: 2015-01-01 through 2022-12-30**, the final eight of thirty calendar years (26.7%). Development: 1993-01-29 through 2014-12-31. No returns at or after 2015-01-01 may be calculated by this cycle. The overlapping historical validation windows of any other family are never inspected.

Construction: at each eligible close hold +1 SPY share-notional to the next open; raw overnight return is `(Open_t + Dividends_t - Close_{t-1})/Close_{t-1}` and raw intraday control is `(Close_t-Open_t)/Open_t`. Ex-date dividends belong to the overnight owner; splits are rejected if nonzero. Equal $1 starting notional per interval, noncompounding return series. Active return is overnight minus intraday (published contrast). The comparison is timing-window matched, not a claim of market-neutrality. Day t is available only after next open/close, so no look-ahead. Execution at official close/open is idealized; closing/opening auction implementation slippage is represented in costs.

Base costs: 2 bp per side of overnight exposure, 4 bp roundtrip; intraday control 2 bp per side, 4 bp roundtrip. Active net subtracts both (8 bp per trading day). Stress doubles these to 16 bp/day. No leverage. Dividend tax not modeled; Canadian taxable-account after-tax return is not asserted.

Inference: daily active net versus zero under Amendment 4's calibrated one-sided AR-sieve/HAC test, B=159, H=1, regime bandwidth fraction 0.20 because the literature and 2026 Fed update document time-varying opportunity; no crash layer (long-equity not short crash insurance). Exposure-matched control is the opposite trading window of the same ETF. Use 252 trading days/year; effective N from A4 autocorrelation formula floored at 1. Stress mean must be positive. Concentration: no single calendar year >40% of cumulative active P&L. Positive only if p < 0.05/K across all actually run candidates, stressed mean positive, and concentration passes. No parameter modification after dev.
