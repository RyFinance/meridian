# Preregistration: aggregate short interest timing

Status: LOCKED BEFORE RETURNS. Research only; not owner-registered or a forward signal.

Source: Rapach, Ringgenberg, and Zhou (2016), *Short Interest and Aggregate Stock Returns*, JFE 121:46–65, PDF pp. 49–50 (aggregate EWSI/SII and release timing), pp. 54–55 (recursive out-of-sample forecasts), and p. 57 Eq. (7) (allocation). https://down.aefweb.net/WorkingPapers/w716.pdf . Author's update: https://sites.google.com/site/ringgenberghomepage/data . The published economic claim is that informed aggregate short sellers forecast negative market cash-flow news, so higher SII forecasts lower excess equity returns. Authors' independently updated workbook includes a recursive `siiOutOfSample` column; full-sample `siiInSample` is excluded.

Universe: U.S. market represented by liquid SPY; one author-provided aggregate SII. Underlying Compustat constituent short-interest records are not free; use of the published recursively detrended index is a declared source-data deviation, not an independently reconstructable raw FINRA measure. SPY inception is 1993. Training/warm-up 1993-01 through 2003-12. Evaluation eligible 2004-01 through 2021-12. **Locked holdout: 2017-01 through 2021-12**, final five of eighteen evaluation years (27.8%, meeting the latest-25%/minimum-five-year rule). Development 2004-01 through 2016-12. No 2017+ return computation this cycle.

Signal: at month-end t use only `siiOutOfSample[t]`, which reflects a recursive point-in-time trend estimate; the paper states mid-month short interest was public four business days after compilation, so it is available by month-end. The author workbook's actual publication/revision timestamps are not supplied; flag this limitation. Regress next-month SPY excess return on lagged SII, recursively using only months through t-1, with at least 120 training observations. One-month horizon is fixed. Forecast variance is the rolling variance of the prior 120 monthly SPY excess returns. As in paper Eq. (7), equity weight `clip(mu_hat/(3*var_hat), -0.5, 1.5)`, rebalanced monthly. The exact-source strategy permits short/levered exposure; this is *not* approved for a retail account. No return-dependent tuning.

Control: paper's prevailing-mean forecast through t-1 passed through the same variance and clipping rule. Raw differential is `(w_SII-w_mean)*SPY_excess`. A4 exposure-matched active further subtracts `mean(w_SII-w_mean)*SPY_excess` over development, isolating timing rather than structural average beta. Base trading cost: 5 bp per unit change in gross SPY exposure for each book; stress 10 bp. Differential subtracts both books' costs. Cash earns the short rate; excess-return comparison cancels cash. SPY raw close-to-close price return plus actual cash dividend provides total return; one-month Treasury-bill proxy is the French RF series from the archived July 2022 data cut, declared deviation from paper's Goyal bill series.

Inference: monthly active net under Amendment 4 calibrated one-sided AR-sieve/HAC p, B=159, H=1, regime bandwidth 0.20 because persistent SII and the paper's potential structural shorting-market change; no crash layer since signal is informational market timing rather than explicit short-volatility premium. Annualization 12 months. A4 effective N and concentration assessed on the monthly portfolio series, not 2 million underlying records. Stress mean positive, no year >40% cumulative active P&L, p < 0.05/K. If authors' recursive SII cannot be verified as actually real-time published, no positive may be claimed without a source-data audit.
