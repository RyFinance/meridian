# Preregistration: time-series factor momentum

Status: LOCKED BEFORE RETURNS. Research-only factor portfolios; not a retail-tradable strategy, owner-registered signal, or forward signal.

Source: Ehsani and Linnainmaa, *Factor Momentum and the Momentum Factor*, Journal of Finance 2022 / NBER working paper 25551. In the working paper pp. 9–10/Table 3, the time-series strategy is long each factor whose prior one-year return is positive and short each factor whose prior one-year return is negative, with monthly rebalancing; each factor is itself long-short. https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w25551/w25551.pdf . Published mechanism: factor return autocorrelation and slow-moving capital; subsequent international evidence is mixed, so post-publication robustness is not assumed.

Universe: four monthly French U.S. factor portfolios SMB, HML, RMW, CMA from the July 2022 archived data cut. Exclude Mkt-RF (not long-short style factor), RF and separate UMD (individual-stock momentum, a spent family). Reduction from the source paper's 20 factors to four is a declared feasibility deviation; it weakens power and breadth. Dataset 1963-07 through 2022-07. Evaluation starts 1990-01 to make the recent economic era the target. **Locked holdout: 2014-01 through 2022-07**, final 8 years 7 months of a 32-year 7-month eligible span (26.3%). Development 1990-01 through 2013-12. No holdout returns computed this cycle.

Signal: for each factor f and month t, sum the **12 completed monthly returns t-12 through t-1** (no skip); position is +1 if sum >0, -1 if <0, and 0 if exactly zero. Form the equal-weight average across the four signed factor portfolios with gross factor notional 1; rebalance monthly. The underlying French factor returns are in percent and divided by 100. No look-ahead or restatement beyond the fixed 2022 archive cut. This is a reduced-universe test of the source's time-series rule, not the source's original 20-factor result.

Control: A4 static exposure-matched four-factor basket with factor-f weight equal to that factor's mean strategy weight in the development window; active equals dynamic book minus static factor return. Base execution cost 10 bp per month for underlying gross exposure plus 5 bp per unit factor-position change, including initial establishment; stress doubles both. This is a rough research-portfolio cost proxy, not a fully modeled constituent-level implementation. If it passes statistically it remains nontradable pending constituent-level execution data.

Inference: monthly active net, Amendment 4 one-sided calibrated AR-sieve/HAC test B=159, H=1, 0.20 regime bandwidth (time-series momentum/regime design), no explicit crash layer because the four zero-investment long-short factors are not an option-selling or funded carry book. Annualization 12. A4 effective N, stress-positive and max-year-40% concentration rules. Positive requires p < 0.05/K across actual run candidates. A single four-factor strategy is one trial; no variant selections.
