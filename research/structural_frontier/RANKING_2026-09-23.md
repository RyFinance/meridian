# Structural-return frontier: ex-ante ranking

Frozen 2026-09-23 before calculating any return for the candidates below. The scores reflect mechanisms and source feasibility, **not their historical performance in this repository**. A score of 1–5 is given in this order: economic mechanism, prior research, reason for persistence, data quality, sample length, realistic tradability, independence from the ledger, cost resilience, portfolio diversification. Total is a triage score, not an expected Sharpe.

Live LSE Terminal/MCP checks this session confirmed: `TLT` options have historical OPRA contract-day prints from 2014 and recent `options_flow` shows prints without bid/ask; `US2Y`/`US10Y` and other government yields are date-level series; `JGB_FUT` is one daily continuous level; `GC.F` is a continuous chart with Sunday bars, not a contract-by-expiry curve. The catalog has 68 futures but most begin in 2025–26. Spot-week FX forward points exist for nine G10 pairs from 2015, not for the emerging crosses. Existing ETF total-return files provide longer, investable histories for Treasury and credit exposure. None of these source checks calculated a candidate return.

Scores rank **eligible new families** first; spent/infeasible rows are shown to document the search and are not candidates regardless of numerical total. The top three were selected before any development return. The three mechanisms carry different primary exposures: duration/inflation, corporate default/liquidity, and rates volatility/insurance. A passing return stream must still clear the existing strict gate, matched controls, centered block-null calibration and stress costs; a modest but genuine 0.5–0.9 Sharpe may be retained as a component rather than optimized upward.
