# Three structural-return streams: preregistered development plan

2026-09-23. Written and registered **before any candidate or control return was computed**. The [ex-ante ranking](RANKING_2026-09-23.md) selected these three economically distinct families. Do not change signs, maturities, option strike, costs, controls or periods after viewing results. Failed families stop; validation remains locked. No capital is traded.

## Prior work and hypotheses

**A. Treasury duration risk premium.** IEF held with fully funded capital earns incremental return versus SHY because investors require compensation for intermediate nominal-rate and inflation risk. This is an *unconditional return stream*, not a yield-curve predictor. Prior `multiasset` tested cross-country 10-year carry ranking from synthetic bond returns; it did not test an investable, unconditional U.S. intermediate-versus-short Treasury return. LSE `bonds/US2Y` and `US10Y` confirm a long date-level yield history but are not used to synthesize trade returns. Source literature: [Cochrane–Piazzesi](https://www.nber.org/papers/w9178), [Federal Reserve term-premium note](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/robustness-of-long-maturity-term-premium-estimates-20170403.html).

**B. Corporate credit/default/liquidity premium.** HYG earns incremental return versus a short/intermediate investment-grade Treasury-bond control because bearing default and dealer-liquidity risk should command compensation. This is *not* the failed HY/IG index-ratio timing rule (`credit_lead`) and does not use the concurrent `credit_gate` credit-to-equity signal. No parameter is conditioned on LSE credit-index performance. LSE `credit_indices/USD_LIQ_HY` and `USD_CORP_1_3` confirm 2010+ date-level index histories; these are not executable prices or test returns. Source literature: [Gilchrist–Zakrajšek](https://www.nber.org/papers/w17021), [Federal Reserve risk-premium discussion](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/stein20140321a.htm).

**C. Treasury-option insurance/variance premium.** A fixed, cash-secured short at-the-money TLT put written monthly earns more than a 50% TLT/50% bill exposure with similar initial equity-rate direction, because long-convexity demand creates an option premium. Distinct underlying and tenor from the repo's failed one-day SPY/QQQ/IWM put-writing test. This is not a claim of riskless theta: rate jumps and 2022 losses belong in the sample. Source literature: [Bond variance risk premia](https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/id/eprint/94008/), [TLT option-selling evidence](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/fut.20423).

## Data semantics audited before returns

- A and B use `data/multiasset/etf/{IEF,SHY,TLT,HYG,LQD,SPY}.parquet`, official-session Yahoo `auto_adjust=True` ETF OHLC; adjusted opens include split/dividend adjustments. Every named file has no missing open/close or duplicate date. IEF/SHY/TLT/SPY start 2003-01-02; HYG starts 2007-04-11. Single surviving ETFs are predefined by hypothesis; there is no current-constituent stock selection. ETF expense ratios are already reflected in returns; historical adjusted prices may be vendor-revised. LSE Terminal confirmed `US2Y`, `US10Y`, `USD_LIQ_HY`, `USD_CORP_1_3` are date-level series with no publication hour/vintages; **none enters a trading signal**.
- C uses LSE `/export` daily OPRA contract trade OHLCV `data/lse/structural_frontier/TLT_options_2014_2022.parquet`: 1,067,797 contract-days across 2,163 dates, 2014-06-02 to 2022-12-30. No duplicate `(ts,osi)`, nonpositive strike/close/volume or missing fields; `ts` is midnight UTC for the trade date, not an executable midnight quote. The archive has **no bid/ask, quote time or open interest**. Raw official-session TLT spot and adjusted closes, dividends and splits are separately archived with `auto_adjust=False` (105 dividend dates, zero splits). Raw close selects the strike and determines put intrinsic value; adjusted TLT close measures the total-return control. LSE `bonds/US3M` annual-percent date-level yield observations feed the bill-collateral accrual with a full ETF-session lag; 17 weekend rows are ignored. Its min/max in the 2014–2022 source pull are −0.046% / 4.457%; it has no historical as-of vintage. Cash-accrual uncertainty is small relative to an option premium but remains a source limitation.
- C source-only calendar QA: for every third-Friday decision from 2014-06 through 2022-12, a next-month monthly put contract existed with **prior-decision-day volume ≥100**, and that exact OSI traded on the next ETF session. All 102 development cycles have a candidate. Option close is the last trade of the entry day; it is **not guaranteed executable at that price**. The frozen base and stress costs below test this, but a development pass alone cannot establish live executability.

## Frozen A: intermediate Treasury holding

- Development: common IEF/SHY/TLT dates 2003-01-02 through 2014-12-31 inclusive. Validation: 2015-01-01 onward, locked. Enter at the first adjusted official open, hold IEF fully funded, mark adjusted open-to-next-open through the last development open. Charge 3 bp at initial purchase and 3 bp at development liquidation; stress 9 bp each. No turnover between. No leverage, borrowing or modelled cash carry; IEF dividends are in adjusted returns.
- `C0` (gate): fully funded SHY buy/hold on identical dates, generously charged **zero** trading cost. The daily active spread is candidate net return minus SHY return. `C1` (diagnostic): fixed 1/3 TLT + 2/3 SHY, rebalanced at each month's first open, zero assumed cost; it roughly matches IEF's duration with a barbell, so a raw IEF win over SHY must not be misrepresented as maturity-specific alpha. Report correlation of active daily returns with SPY minus SHY, year-by-year attribution, worst 12/24-month removal and drawdown.

## Frozen B: high-yield credit holding

- Development: common HYG/LQD/SHY/SPY dates 2008-01-02 through 2016-12-30 inclusive, deliberately including the 2008 crisis. Validation: 2017-01-01 onward, locked. Enter HYG at first adjusted official open, hold fully funded, mark adjusted open-to-next-open through the last development open. Charge 5 bp initial and 5 bp liquidation; stress 15 bp each. No leverage or borrow. Dividends and fund fees are embedded in adjusted total returns.
- `C0` (gate): 1/3 LQD + 2/3 SHY, rebalanced at each month's first open with **zero** cost; this is an approximate duration-matched investment-grade bond alternative (not a perfect duration hedge). `C1` (gate): 1/4 SPY + 3/4 SHY rebalanced at each month's first open with **zero** cost, to challenge generic equity-credit beta. The candidate must have positive base-cost average return versus C1 as well as the strict C0 gate. Report regression beta of HYG-minus-C0 on SPY-minus-SHY, year-by-year attribution, worst 12/24-month removal and drawdown. If it passes, audit actual historical ETF durations and equity beta before validation.

## Frozen C: monthly TLT put insurance

- Development decisions: June 2014 through November 2022. Validation decisions: December 2022 onward, locked. For each calendar month's third Friday (or most recent TLT trading session within the preceding two days if that Friday is a market holiday), decide after the official close using that session's **raw** TLT close and LSE option contracts/volume. Eligible puts have expiry within ±1 calendar day of the *next* month's third Friday and decision-day volume ≥100. Choose the expiry nearest that Friday (tie: earlier expiry), then strike nearest the decision close (tie: lower strike). The contract/OSI is frozen at decision time. On the **next TLT session**, sell exactly that OSI using its LSE trade-day `close`; if absent, hold only bills for the cycle. No same-day option volume or option price informs selection. The option's exit is intrinsic value at its expiry's last TLT official close on or before the contractual expiration date. Option and control share entry-close-to-expiry-close dates. Cycles do not overlap. No cash-flow reinvestment before expiry.
- Fully cash-secured notional is `strike × 100` per contract, with one unit of collateral per unit of strike notional. A cycle portfolio return is accrued Treasury-bill return plus `(entry option premium − expiry intrinsic payoff − option costs) / strike`. Bill accrual uses the **latest LSE US3M yield dated before each ETF session**, compounding at `(yield_percent/100) × calendar_days/365` between closes; reject observations older than seven calendar days. This is a bill-ladder proxy, not a funded options margin account. No leverage.
- Base option execution cost: $0.05 per share deducted from the sale print plus $0.65 per 100-share contract commission; if assigned in the money, deduct $0.05/share for immediate share liquidation plus another $0.65. Stress: $0.15/share and $1.00/contract for the same sides. These are *assumed* slippages because LSE has prints, not quotes. Early American assignment is not modelled; assess it as an implementation limitation if the rule passes.
- `C0` (gate): 50% TLT adjusted total return + 50% lagged-yield bill accrual over identical cycles, zero trading cost. This is a fixed approximate initial-delta exposure control. `C1`: bills alone; candidate must earn positive base-cost mean excess over C1. `C2`: 100% TLT total return, descriptive. Report short-put tail/worst cycle, share assigned, raw premium and payoff, year effects, and correlation with the A and B active monthly streams if any survive. Do not alter the 50% control based on development delta estimates.

## Gate, null calibration, and validation lock

- One candidate per family; **three** planned tests. Every development candidate requires (i) net C0-active excess Sharpe ≥ `1.8/sqrt(T)` with T in calendar years of its development window, (ii) stress-cost C0-active annualized mean > 0, and (iii) a one-sided centered circular-block bootstrap p-value for C0-active mean < **0.05/3 = 0.0166667**. B additionally needs positive base mean versus C1; C additionally needs positive base mean versus cash C1. The Bonferroni threshold treats the three planned tests as one research batch. Report an effect estimate and interval even if a gate fails; a 0.5–0.9 Sharpe is worth retaining only if it clears all controls/nulls.
- For A/B, the null draws 5,000 resamples of **paired daily** candidate/control returns in 21-session circular blocks, seed 20260923. For C, it draws paired cycle returns in three-cycle circular blocks. For the zero-premium null, center each observed active comparison by its own development mean before resampling, preserving volatility, serial dependence and paired correlation. Retain the cost difference when evaluating stress worlds. Report P(full development pass|null) with Monte-Carlo SE and the observed p-value. Because unconditional premia have no signal, **circularly shifting a signal is inapplicable**; this is the null-world analogue of the project's `research.null_lab` procedure.
- No validation input is read before the associated registered dev gate passes and a separate `VALIDATION_UNLOCK.md` with frozen code/input hashes is registered. Validation requires net C0-active Sharpe ≥0.75, stress active mean >0, one-sided 21-day (A/B) or three-cycle (C) block p<0.05/3, and any family-specific C1 gate. A development pass is a *candidate portfolio component*, never deployment approval. For C, absent historical executable quotes may keep the verdict **inconclusive even after a numeric pass**.
- If a family fails, record it and stop. Do not reverse, change the control, window, costs, option tenor/strike or add a timing filter. No external paid data, trades, production changes, commit or push.
