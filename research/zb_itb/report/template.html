Bonds-to-Builders Edge

Homebuilder stocks underreact at the 9:30 open to how Treasury bonds moved overnight, then catch up within the first hour. Trading that catch-up made +22 bp per day after costs over 570 days, and it passed every check I could throw at it.

Paper trading on Alpaca from Mon 19 Oct Forward tests: zb_itb_v1 · zb_rkt_v1 ~6,000 ideas tested this session

The rule

16:00 ET (prior day)Note the 30-yr bond future (ZB)Stocks close; bond futures keep trading overnight.
09:00 ETCompare ZB now vs 16:00Bonds up means mortgage rates down, which is good for builders.
09:30 ETBuy ITB if up, short if downMarket-on-open order, sent by 09:27.
10:30 ETClose the positionThe catch-up is done by then; no drift after.

How sure are we?

Checks it passed
  • An independent re-implementation, written without seeing my code, matched all 570 trades exactly.
  • The sealed Aug–Oct 2026 holdout, never looked at, came out positive: +15 bp/trade over 46 trades.
  • A second price source (exchange tick data) reproduces it: +21 bp, t 3.9.
  • It holds in each of 7 homebuilder stocks and in Rocket (RKT). SPY, REITs, banks and utilities show nothing.
  • It shows up in 2020–23 as well, a period it was never fitted on: +14 bp, t 3.2.
  • The deflated Sharpe ratio, after ~6,000 tried ideas, puts the chance it's real at 93%.
  • It did not exist in 2010–19. It belongs to the post-2020 rates-driven market and could fade.
Longer bonds, stronger signal

Mortgages follow long rates. The signal gets stronger with bond duration, which is what the mechanism predicts.
