# Lock rule for the locked crypto_funding_xs validation window (from the cycle-5 C2 design)

Status: DRAFT until its hash is in `research/preregistrations.jsonl`. Written 2026-09-25 by the director. This rule
only restricts what may be done; it opens nothing.

## Rule

While `crypto_funding_xs` validation (2023-01-01 → 2026-08-31) is locked, or has been opened but not yet run:
- **Forbidden:** computing, viewing or reporting any cross-sectional funding-sorted return, or any funding→return
  statistic, for any day in that window. This applies on ANY venue or source: Binance, Bybit, OKX, BitMEX,
  Hyperliquid, dYdX, Kraken, Deribit, aggregators and third-party dashboards.
- **Allowed:** metadata-only work, such as listing and delisting dates, file counts and completeness checks.
  Collectors may store raw snapshots, but nobody may compute a funding-sorted return from them for that window.

## When the lock lifts

Only when either:
- the Binance validation result is filed; or
- the director registers that the validation is permanently forfeited, in which case the window is recorded as
  contaminated for this claim for good.

## Breaches

A breach is logged in the ledger. If the Binance validation is run after a breach, it is labelled "contaminated".

## Why

Cross-venue funding ranks and returns are nearly the same draw, with estimated strategy-return correlation 0.5–0.9
(`reports/crypto_replication_design/REPORT.md` §1). Seeing another venue's 2023–26 result would reveal most of the
locked Binance validation statistic.
