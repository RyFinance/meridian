# LSE high-yield versus investment-grade credit information for equity/bond rotation

2026-09-23. This plan is to be registered before computing any strategy or control return. One hypothesis, one signal horizon and one portfolio. Development-only LSE credit levels have been downloaded for coverage and source checks; no credit signal was paired with a forward asset return before registration.

## Novelty and mechanism

The ledger's multiasset book used ETF price premia and its return-search ML feature used a 63-day **LQD/IEF** trend (investment-grade credit against Treasuries). No registered experiment used LSE `credit_indices`, the **high-yield versus investment-grade** index relationship, or that relationship as an equity/bond allocation signal. A deteriorating high-yield market reflects funding/default risk and constrained credit investors; that stress may persist into subsequent equity returns. This is a different information set from SPY's own trend and from LQD/IEF duration exposure. The study must beat both simple price-only and buyable-credit proxies to support an incremental information claim.

## Source semantics and risks, audited before returns

- Use LSE Terminal MCP `macro_series`, `dataset="credit_indices"`, symbols `USD_LIQ_HY` and `USD_LIQ_IG`. Catalog names are USD liquid high-yield and investment-grade **corporate-bond index levels**, unit `index`, daily, 2010 onward. These are not spread/yield observations. The source field is blank, observations have dates but no publication hours or as-of vintages, and index constituents/reconstitution are not supplied.
- Archive raw 2010–2020 MCP replies, derived parquets and SHA-256 manifest in `data/lse/credit_lead/`. The development extract contains 2,829 HY and 2,759 IG dates; 15 weekend rows each, and 71 HY-only dates. Keep only dates shared by HY, IG and the SPY/IEF official trading calendar. Values must be positive, finite, unique by date. A source value is **eligible only after a full intervening US trading session**: on the first ETF trading day of month m, the newest usable credit date is the second-to-last ETF trading day of month m−1. If the latest shared credit date is more than 3 ETF sessions older, use the prespecified neutral allocation.
- The source may be historically corrected. This precludes a point-in-time claim until an independent vintage or contemporaneous archived source is checked. The lag reduces publication-hour leakage but cannot rule out retrospective revisions. A 2010–2020 same-day integrity check found broad, imperfect correspondence with adjusted ETF prices: IG/LQD daily-return correlation 0.73 and HY/HYG 0.68. This check is about source identity, not forecasting.
- Execution and control prices come from existing `data/multiasset/etf/{SPY,IEF,HYG,LQD}.parquet`, yfinance `auto_adjust=True`, with split/dividend-adjusted official-session OHLC. ETF constituents can change, but the assets themselves existed before the test and have no current-stock-universe survivorship issue. Adjusted opens are historical accounting approximations to total-return open fills; any passing outcome needs raw-price/dividend and executable-fill replication.

## Frozen signal, trading and controls

- Decision date: first common SPY/IEF US trading day of each calendar month. Let t be the latest common eligible LSE credit date at or before the previous month's second-to-last SPY/IEF trading day. Let t−20 be the 20th preceding **common eligible credit date**. Signal `s = log(HY_t/IG_t) − log(HY_(t−20)/IG_(t−20))`. Allocate 100% to SPY if s > 0, otherwise 100% to IEF. Zero is IEF. If 21 common credit levels or a fresh t are unavailable, allocate 50% SPY / 50% IEF. No thresholds or optimized weights.
- Hold until the next monthly decision. Daily marked total returns use adjusted ETF **open-to-next-open** prices. On a rebalance open, charge 3 bp per side per dollar traded: `0.0003 × (|Δw_SPY|+|Δw_IEF|)`, including the initial buy. Stress is 9 bp per side. No leverage, shorts, borrow or incremental financing; ETF fees are already in prices. No return crosses the development/validation boundary.
- `C0` primary exposure-matched control: static SPY weight equal to the strategy's **development daily-weighted SPY exposure fraction**, IEF otherwise, starting with the same initial cost. For each shift-null world, recompute its own matched fraction from that world's exposure path; this removes beta/frequency as a source of false passes. Freeze the real development fraction for future validation. Also report a fixed 50/50 SPY/IEF investable benchmark.
- `C1` price-only timing control: identical monthly 100% SPY/IEF rotation and costs, based on sign of SPY's 20-US-session adjusted-close return observed by the same cutoff date. `C2` buyable credit control: identical rotation based on 20-US-session log return of HYG/LQD adjusted-close ratio by that cutoff. A missing control signal uses 50/50. These controls expose whether any benefit is merely equity momentum or publicly buyable credit momentum.
- `P1` reverse allocation (1−SPY weight) on the same dates, costs and fills is a directional placebo, reported only. `P2` 5,000 circular shifts of the monthly LSE signal/allocation path by at least 12 months, with seed 20260923, preserves exposure mix and signal persistence while breaking timing. Use `research.null_lab.api.run_null` in development-only mode with an exactness test of the custom engine. No validation prices enter the null.

## Windows and frozen gates

- Warm-up: 2010–2011 source and ETF observations. **Development:** first January 2012 ETF open through the final ETF open in December 2020, without using a January 2021 open. T is calendar years from 2012-01-01 through 2020-12-31, divided by 365.25. **Validation:** 2021-01 through 2026-09, locked; no validation credit observations or returns are to be loaded now.
- Development primary series is the strategy's daily net return **minus C0's daily net return**. Pass only if its base-cost active Sharpe ≥ `1.8/sqrt(T)` and its stress-cost active annualized mean > 0. Also require positive base-cost annualized mean versus **both C1 and C2**. The custom null gate must apply these same three conditions, and report P(full development pass | null) ± Monte-Carlo SE. A bootstrap p against C0, C1 and C2 is reported diagnostically, not used to rescue a failure.
- If development passes, freeze the measured C0 weight and code, audit source vintages, timing, revised history, benchmark prices, beta, concentration (best contiguous 12/24 months removed), regimes, and costs before registering a validation unlock. The validation pass, if ever unlocked, requires base-cost C0-active Sharpe ≥ 0.75, stress C0-active mean > 0, one-sided 21-day circular-block bootstrap p < 0.05 for C0-active mean, and positive net mean versus C1 and C2. One candidate, no multiple variants.
- If development fails, preserve the result and leave validation locked. Do not reverse, shorten, lengthen or threshold this credit signal as a rescue. A separate independent information family may be studied under a new registration.
