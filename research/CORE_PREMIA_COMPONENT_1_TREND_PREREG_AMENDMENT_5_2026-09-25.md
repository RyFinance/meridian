## Paper provenance and exact rule

Primary: [Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen, “Time Series Momentum,” JFE 104 (2012), §2.4 p.233, §3.2 pp.233–234, Eq. 5 and §4.1 p.236](https://fairmodel.econ.yale.edu/ec439/mosk.pdf). Signal at each month-end `t`: sign of the contract's cumulative **excess return** over the immediately preceding 12 months, including month `t` and excluding no recent month. Hold for month `t+1`; `+1` long, `−1` short, exact zero flat (declared paper-silent default). For eligible instrument `i`, gross paper-fidelity exposure is `sign_i,t × 0.40 / σ_i,t`, where `σ_i,t` is the ex-ante annualized volatility formed from information through the decision close. Portfolio factor return is the **equal average** of these instrument returns across all historically available eligible instruments, not a selected winner set. Produce analogous factors for each of the four classes: equity-index futures, government-bond futures, commodity futures and currency forwards. This is the paper's 12-month/one-month construction; [Hurst, Ooi & Pedersen (2017), p.16](https://fairmodel.econ.yale.edu/ec439/hurst.pdf) supports breadth and cost scrutiny but its 1/3/12-month blend is **not** component 1.

`σ²_i,t = 261·Σ_{j≥0}(1−δ)δ^j(r_i,d−j−μ_i,t)^2`, with `δ=60/61`, daily *excess* return `r`, and EW daily mean `μ` using the same weights; truncate only at the first available observation and renormalize remaining weights. At least **756 valid earlier daily observations** are required before the first signal; no zero/negative-volatility exposure. This three-year warm-up is a researcher default, not a paper parameter. No floor or aggregate cap is applied to the **gross fidelity shadow**. The executable net book uses the same signals/exposures, subject only to explicitly reported capacity/2× combined-book cap, which must not be hidden in the fidelity comparison.

## Accounting, controls and fidelity gate

Keep a gross **paper-fidelity shadow** and separate USD **net costed/proxy-executable** NAV. On a fixed **USD 100,000 research NAV** (not an account assertion), charge contemporaneous half-spread per traded side if independently recorded; otherwise use the declared fixed half-spread default per side: equity/bond ETF **1 bp**, G10 FX **2 bp**, commodity ETF **5 bp**. Use documented commission/exchange fees if available, otherwise **1 bp per traded side**; charge both roll legs and impact `0.10 × daily volatility × sqrt(order notional / prior 20-day ADV notional)` per side when proxy ADV exists, otherwise **2 bp per side**. Reject ETF orders exceeding 1% of prior 20-day ADV. All fallback charges are pre-return deviations, disclosed per market in the feasibility audit, never inferred from our P&L. Cash earns `max(0, 3-month T-bill yield − 0.50 percentage points)`; borrowing/financed exposure costs 3-month T-bill + 2 points or a higher documented actual charge. Embedded ETF expenses are not double-charged. Stress variable trading costs at 2× and 3× and financing +2 percentage points. Separate return, collateral, realized roll/trading costs and NAV ledgers; reconcile monthly and daily marks. These numerical cost/capacity choices are researcher defaults, never selected from returns.

Before returns, snapshot the [AQR Time Series Momentum monthly factors](https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Datasets/Time-Series-Momentum-Factors-Monthly) and definitions. Match gross monthly excess returns by date, USD convention and factor scale; no regression-fitted rescaling. Overall and each of four class columns are **primary**. On identical months compute Pearson `ρ`, OLS-with-intercept `β`, `TE=√12·sd(rep−AQR)`, and `TE/σ_AQR`. All must pass: overall `n≥120`, `ρ≥0.80`, `0.80≤β≤1.20`, `TE/σ_AQR≤0.75`; each class `n≥60`, `ρ≥0.65`, `0.70≤β≤1.30`, `TE/σ_AQR≤0.90`. Different vendor vintages and roll conventions justify tolerance, but require strong co-movement and scale. Missing/unmatched column is **INDETERMINATE**, not PASS; failure is not rescued by changing the signal. Estimate uncertainty with 10,000 stationary-bootstrap draws, seed `20260925`, mean block 12 months; fixed point estimates gate.

The exposure-matched control is always long the **same eligible instruments** at the same `0.40/σ` scale and equal averaging, with identical next-session execution, rolls and applicable costs. Preserve common-month dependence in block inference; no asset-independent resampling.
