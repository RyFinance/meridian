# Amendment 2: H1 volume is exempt from the split rule

Written 2026-09-18 12:49 PDT. Still no signal–return statistic, portfolio return or ranking of names
had been computed. The only numbers seen were one name's daily signal levels, checked for data
quality (AAPL, development window).

Finding: applied to H1, the split rule of amendment 1.1 deletes almost all option volume for months
after a split, because trading stays concentrated in pre-listed expiries. AAPL's option volume on
2014-06-12, three days after its 7:1 split, falls from about 40 million shares a day to 77,700. O/S
would then drop towards zero for reasons that have nothing to do with the hypothesis, and put the
stock in the long leg.

Change: H1's option volume counts every contract that passes the general price and volume filters,
split-adjusted series included (volume is activity, whatever the deliverable). The split rule still
applies to H2 and H3, where adjusted prices would corrupt implied volatility. Nothing else changes.
