# HYPOTHETICAL HOLDINGS: research sample, not a recommendation

These files are generated by `python -m research.autopilot.make_samples`. Do not edit them by hand. They are the
inputs of the sample reports in `reports/cycle9/autopilot/samples/`.

- `config_<config>_<account>.json`: the sample configs. Each carries the label above, which the report prints under
  its title.
- `hypothetical_holdings_<config>_<account>.json`: holdings that never existed. CAD 100,000 was put at target on
  2025-08-29 using real closes from the frozen cycle-8 view, then carried to a holdings file dated 2026-08-31 with
  the same positions and no flows. A margin loan accrues the cycle-8 margin rate (policy rate + 1.5%) monthly, and
  the funds' actual per-share distributions over the year are paid as cash (USD-listed funds net of 15% US
  withholding); cash earns nothing.
- `*_refusal_stale.json` and `*_refusal_margin.json` produce the two refusal examples: STALE prices (a report dated
  2026-09-08) and a TFSA holdings file showing a margin loan.

Prices come from `data/cycle9/autopilot_prices/store`, version `autopilot_prices_20260831`. Nothing here is a
recommendation to trade, and nothing is sent to any broker.
